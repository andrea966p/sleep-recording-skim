## 🔴 The retraction

Yesterday evening I reported that the arm C tether swap **harmed** D2: EMG 10/15 → 0/40 below
0.5 MOhm, p = 8.4e-08, with drift and anaesthesia both excluded. I flagged it provisional when D2
recovered 40 minutes later and said the recording would settle it.

It did, and the conclusion was backwards.

![[2026-08-07-figD-D2-before-after.png]]

| D2 EEG | before the swap | after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| usable | **0.0%** | **87.9%** |
| median 60 Hz | 916.9 µV | **133.2 µV** |
| slope | −0.38, bare-wire-like | **−2.05** |
| railed | 29.7% | **0.0%** |

**The 40 sweep block spanned 21 minutes and the channel was in one open state for all of it.**
Drift and anaesthesia were correctly excluded. **State persistence was not, and could not be from
a 21 minute window.**

> [!important] The rule I am writing down so this does not happen again
> **Impedance blocks screen. Only a multi-hour recording is an outcome.**
> Always state a block's **duration** next to its duty cycle. Never conclude harm or benefit from
> a block alone. A 40 sweep block gave p = 8.4e-08 for a conclusion that was exactly wrong.

## ⭐ What it means scientifically

D2 was tethered on **30 July, 8 days after surgery**, well short of our 14 day protocol, and it
had 0.0% usable EEG yesterday. After a new tether chain it is at 87.9%.

That was the **pre-registered disambiguation** written yesterday: if D2 jumped, tether
construction is sufficient and recovery time is not the driver. It jumped.

> **Long recovery is not required. The chain is sufficient.** The existing cohort is recoverable
> **without new surgery**, and the fix is an engineering change we can apply to every animal.

⚠️ Four things changed together in the swap: tether construction, slip ring removal, connector
re-seating and one isoflurane episode. **"Does the new chain help" is answered. "Is it the slip
ring" still is not**, and cannot be without another anaesthesia per animal.

⭐ Worth noting: **arm B improved B1 with the slip ring in place, arm C improved D2 without it.**
Both new chains worked. What they share is **soldered, resin-encapsulated construction**, which is
now the leading candidate for the active ingredient.

# Evening: the whole cohort was swapped out

Full write-up: [[2026-08-07-ecg-cohort-baseline]].

We pulled every mouse and connected **7 brand new ones**, this time with **three bipolar
channels each, electroencephalogram (EEG), electromyogram (EMG) and electrocardiogram (ECG)**.
Channel order per port is now `/1` EEG, `/2` EMG, `/3` ECG for the first mouse and `/33`, `/34`,
`/35` for the second. Sample rate unchanged at 1000 Hz. **The old B1 mouse stays in port A slot
2 as a control**, still on its short soldered tether, because it is the best channel this
project has ever had.

New on the animal side: **22 cm 8 conductor tether, thicker, wires NOT soldered, thin
heatshrink**, and the **ground tissue wire now comes from the cerebellum instead of right
parietal**.

## What I did, in order

1. Re-ran `remap_channels.py`. **Map unchanged**, 256 channels, 4 ports.
2. Looked at **120 seconds of the live stream** before touching anything.
3. Stopped streaming, ran a **30 sweep impedance block**, 17.4 minutes.
4. Started the **overnight recording**, `allego_0__uid0807-19-14-58`, 19:14:58.

## The stream said one thing

⭐ **The 60 Hz mains hum collapsed across the whole fleet.** Old cohort open channels sat at 800
to 5500 µV. Now only 4 of 24 channels are high and the rest are 20 to 210 µV.

🔴 **But no biology came with it.** After notching the mains and bandpassing 0.5 to 45 Hz, 22 of
24 channels sit at 2 to 6 µV median, which is amplifier input noise. Three independent checks
agreed: spectral slope inside the bare wire range, **not one ECG channel has a detectable
heartbeat** (interval coefficient of variation 0.31 to 0.46 everywhere, a real mouse heart gives
under 0.1), and residual cross correlation under 0.5 everywhere.

⭐ **The control A/33 was the sole exception**, slope -1.93 and 85 µV of real signal, matching
how it behaved in the 23.66 hour run. **So the rig, the amplifiers and my analysis are fine. The
problem is on the animal side of the connector.**

## The impedance said the opposite thing

Floor check passed, 3.4% of 3120 unwired amplifier inputs below 0.5 MΩ. Then:

⭐⭐ **19 of 21 new mouse channels beat the floor, seven of them at 100% duty with zero flips.**
On 2026-08-06 only 4 of 14 channels managed that. **By impedance this is the best the rig has
ever looked.**

🔴 **And the control reads 0.0% on all three sites while being the only slot with real EEG.**
That part is explainable, B1's EEG median was already 0.813 MΩ, above the 0.5 MΩ line.
⚠️ **THE 0.5 MΩ CRITERION CALLS OUR BEST EVER CHANNEL OPEN. It beats an unwired floor, it is not
an absolute measure of contact, do not quote it as one.**

## One hypothesis explains all three observations

⭐⭐ **The bipolar pairs may be shorted somewhere in the unsoldered 8 conductor tether.** A short
would give very low impedance (several sites read 4 to 20 kΩ, at or below the published 10 to
50 kΩ range for epidural electrodes), excellent mains rejection so almost no hum, and **zero
differential signal by construction**. That is exactly the three things we measured.

⚠️ **HYPOTHESIS, NOT A RESULT.** Crimped under thin heatshrink is precisely the construction
that makes conductor to conductor shorts, and both chains that ever worked here were soldered
and resin encapsulated. But **120 seconds cannot settle it and neither can a 17 minute impedance
block**, that is the lesson I had to learn this morning. **The overnight recording is the test.**

## Also found, a library bug

🔴 `AllegoClient.set_recording_config(...)` **is broken**, it throws
`StatusCode.INTERNAL "Exception serializing request!"` because the `ConfigRecording` protobuf has
a required `recordingType` enum that the library never sets. Workaround and the exact code are in
the experiment note. ⚠️ **A failed config leaves the old output path in place silently, and the
`path` field in `get_status().recording` is useless, it reports the calling process's working
directory. Always `ls` the destination and check `_data.xdat` is growing.**

## ☐ 2. Run the DMM continuity test on the EEG path

⭐ **The free tier-1 bench test. It has been owed since 2026-08-02 and nothing
should be concluded about the implants until it is done.**

Full procedure, with safety, expected readings and a recording sheet:
**[[dmm-eeg-path-continuity-test]]**.

- ☐ A1 — EMG perfect (0.087 MΩ), EEG open the full 27.9 h. Cleanest case
- ☐ D1 — same pattern (EMG 0.202 MΩ). Independent replication
- ☐ B1 — the mirror: best EEG in the fleet (0.133 MΩ), dead EMG

The question it answers: **do the two EEG wires actually reach the board?** Pads
2 and 3 (EMG) are the built-in control, because on A1 and D1 they are proven
working by 27.9 hours of clean EMG through the same board, the same slip ring
and the same tether.

**Before the first probe touches anything:** acquisition stopped and software
closed · XDAQ powered down · **tether unplugged at the slip ring**, so no test
current can reach an animal · **headstage lifted off the C3410 board**, so the
meter is not driving the RHD2216 inputs · probes touched together to prove the
meter is alive.

⭐ **Do not skip the rotation test.** Our failure is intermittent (contact has
been seen flipping 522 times in half a second) and a static probe cannot see it.
Hold the beep on a pad and turn the slip ring slowly through 360°. Any flicker
is a positive finding, and if pads 0/1 flicker where 2/3 do not, **the slip ring
is the fault and the implants are exonerated**.

- ☐ record results into the sheet in §4.6 of the procedure, one block per animal
- ☐ photograph all four pads under magnification before touching anything with an iron
- ☐ after any repair: re-sweep impedance and log it in [[impedance-sweep-register]]
  with a `config-test` context label

> [!danger] Still forbidden
> No "reseating" of electrodes or EMG wires — impossible after implantation. No
> tether change without a written argument (isoflurane, ruins the batch). No
> surgeries. The free tier gets exhausted first.

Eight newly operated mice from batch **W-SD** went on the rig and I took a short recording,

these animals as **W-SD plus the rig position**, so W-SD A1, W-SD A2 and so on.

⚠️ **THESE ARE EIGHT BRAND NEW ANIMALS AND NOTHING FROM ANY EARLIER COHORT CARRIES OVER.** A rig
position is a socket on the amplifier, not an identity, so "D2" today and "D2" in an older note
are different mice. Every judgement was made against wire free amplifier inputs recorded inside
this same file.

**Five of the eight give clean brain signal: W-SD A1, A2, B2, C1 and D2, all at 100 % of epochs
with zero railing.** Four of the five also give a believable muscle signal; **W-SD B2 has
electroencephalogram (EEG) only**, its electromyogram (EMG) channel sits at 475 µV with 640 µV of
mains hum and is open. **W-SD C2 has an open EEG electrode**, quiet and spectrally flat at the
same time, which is the signature of an input attached to nothing.

Full numbers, tables and the reasoning: [[2026-08-18-W-SD-cohort-first-screen]].

### 🔴 Two headstages lost their ground TODAY

**W-SD B1 and W-SD D1 cannot be judged at all**, because the unconnected inputs on their own chips
have gone silent: 162 µV and 82 µV of 60 Hz, against 3000 to 5200 µV on the other six chips. A
grounded chip sees the room's mains hum on its bare inputs; a chip with no ground rejects mains as
common mode and goes quiet. Their control has stopped being a control.

Comparing the same table across the last four recordings shows the silent chip was **port A slot 2
on 14 and 16 August**, and today port A slot 2 is normal while B slot 1 and D slot 1 have gone
silent instead. **The fault follows whatever was last unplugged**, which makes it the same ground
pin problem as [[2026-08-07]] and a connector re mate, with no anaesthesia. Andrea is checking the
seating tomorrow morning.

🔑 **Verify that fix on the 60 Hz of the wire free inputs, NOT on an impedance sweep.** A floating
channel reads a beautifully low and stable impedance.

### ⚠️ The standard engine got this file wrong

`core/criteria.py` pools all 112 wire free sites in a file into one floor and takes the 1st
percentile. With two chips floating, their quiet steep inputs dragged the pooled threshold to a
spectral slope of −2.002, which is steeper than every animal channel, so the pooled run scored the
whole cohort at 0 to 13 % usable and would have condemned five good mice.

Rerunning with a **per chip floor**, where each animal is judged only against the fourteen bare
inputs on its own port and slot, gives the result above, and every chip passes its own
falsification check. **The pooled floor is unsafe the moment any one headstage floats, and it
fails silently**, because the falsification check asks whether the floor scores usable rather than
whether the chips agree with each other.

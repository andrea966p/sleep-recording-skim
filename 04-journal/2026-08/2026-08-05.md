## Plan for today: the tether comparison

Full design: [[2026-08-05-tether-comparison]]. Yesterday: [[2026-08-04]].

Two new tethers built, **both soldered joints under resin** instead of thin heatshrink:
1. **short standard**, still through the slip ring
2. **long custom**, plugs onto the mouse and goes **straight to the adapter board, no slip ring**

Because both are built the same way, **arm C against arm B is a clean single variable
test of the slip ring**. That is why the long tether goes last.

### Decisions taken before starting

- **Manual sweeps.** No loop script today. Caps sample size at 20 per arm, which caps power.
- **No cameras.** Pis and cameras stay removed until further notice ⇒ **no movement covariate**,
  so today says nothing about the movement hypothesis and must not try to.
- **B1 is the test animal.** Only animal with a proven good electrode (0.052 MΩ), maximum
  volatility (166× in 23 min), baseline duty cycle near 50%, and port already discharged
  as a confound.
- **D2 stays plugged into port D, untouched, all session.** A sweep reads every port at
  once, so this is a free concurrent control. **Do not swap its tether.**
- **D1 is the backup** if B1's baseline comes out 0/20 or 20/20.
- A1 EMG, C1 EMG, C2 EMG rejected: connected in all four historical sweeps ⇒ at the
  **ceiling**, cannot show improvement.

### ⭐ Pre-registered predictions, written BEFORE arm A

| if the fault is | then |
| --- | --- |
| tether joints | B ≫ A, C ≈ B |
| the slip ring | C ≫ B, B ≈ A |
| both | A < B < C, monotone |
| neither, ie headcap or implant interface | all three ≈ 50%, D2 solid throughout |
| measurement or room | D2 churns too, no arm comparison means anything |

Row four is a likely outcome and **is not a failure**. It would clear the tether and the
slip ring by measurement rather than by assumption.

Primary outcome is **duty cycle**, the fraction of 20 sweeps reading \|Z\| < 2 MΩ. At a
~50% flip rate a single reading is a coin toss and cannot be used for anything.

**Power, accepted in advance:** 20 sweeps per arm detects 50% → 90% (p ≈ 0.008) but **not**
50% → 80% (p ≈ 0.10). An arm landing in that middle band gets **extended**, not argued about.

## Work Log

### Pi Physical Disturbance Incident

Workers came in to mount a shelf and disconnected the ethernet cable from the router. Since the Pi is powered via PoE (Power over Ethernet), disconnecting the ethernet cable = cutting power. This caused an unclean shutdown.

**Timeline:**
- Feb 12: Frigate deployed successfully, Pi running perfectly for 2+ hours with both cameras streaming to Frigate
- Workers disconnected ethernet from router (unintentional power cut to Pi)
- Pi rebooted, SSH initially failed (network still disrupted)
- Tried a different ethernet cable — Pi booted but shut down after ~1 minute
- Reverted to original cable — Pi stable again
- Ran full 12-step diagnosis twice, everything healthy both times

**Second cable failure analysis:** The 1-minute shutdown timing aligns with `ExecStartPre=/bin/sleep 60` in go2rtc.service. Pi boots fine on the new cable, then 60s later go2rtc starts both cameras + IR LEDs, and the power draw may exceed what that cable can deliver through PoE. However, on a third attempt the new cable worked fine — may have been a loose connection rather than cable inadequacy.

### Persistent Journal Fix (Third Attempt)

The persistent journal config from 2026-02-11 was **empty** — the file existed at `/etc/systemd/journald.conf.d/99-persistent-journal.conf` but had no content. Likely caused by the unclean shutdown during the original write, or the file was created but content never flushed to SD card.

**Fix applied:**
```
[Journal]
  Storage=persistent
  SystemMaxUse=500M
```

**Verification:**
- `systemd-analyze cat-config` shows `Storage=persistent` as final override
- `journalctl --flush` created machine-id directory `55ef14fe...` under `/var/log/journal/`
- 16MB of logs on disk
- Next crash will be captured via `journalctl -b -1`

### Crash Canary Deployed

Logs: temp, throttle, load, memory, go2rtc status, rpicam-vid count/CPU, go2rtc memory, OOM kill count. Auto-rotates at 5MB.

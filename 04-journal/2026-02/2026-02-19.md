## Work Log

### Pi Found Dead After Holiday

Returned after 6 days (Lunar New Year). Pi was unresponsive — no ping, no SSH. Found it with red LED on, green LED off. Manual power cycle brought it back.

### Diagnosis

Used `journalctl --list-boots` and crash-canary logs to determine the Pi died on **Feb 13 at 10:40** — less than a day after the last session. It was dead the entire holiday week. All software metrics were perfectly normal at the moment of death (no thermal, memory, CPU, or throttling issues).

Full details: [[2026-02-19-pmic-fault-poe-death]]

### Root Cause

Missing `PSU_MAX_CURRENT` in eeprom config. The Pi 5 PMIC didn't know the PoE HAT could deliver enough current, so after a power glitch it entered a fault state and refused to reboot. Known issue with Waveshare PoE HATs — [GitHub thread](https://github.com/geerlingguy/raspberry-pi-pcie-devices/issues/597).

### Fixes Applied

```
PSU_MAX_CURRENT=5000    # Tell PMIC the PoE HAT can deliver 5A
POWER_OFF_ON_HALT=1     # Auto power-cycle on halt (PoE brings it right back)
```

Eeprom firmware updated from May 2025 to Dec 2025.

## Issues

### No Remote Recovery Option
Unmanaged PoE switch (NEXT-POE3008GF) means no way to remotely power-cycle the Pi. A managed PoE switch or ping-based alerting from the NVR would prevent multi-day outages.

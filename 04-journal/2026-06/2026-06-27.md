## ROOT CAUSE FOUND — Setup B railing = animal has NO ground tie (not a cold joint)

Railing of the detected 2-mice headstage (10/12 ch at 10–12.6mV pk-pk, both mice railed together) is **NOT** the freshly-soldered GND-pad joint and **NOT** the busbar. Bench continuity proved the system-side ground chain is solid. The real cause: **no electrode carried headstage GND into the animal → animal floats → bipolar amp common-mode drives every channel into saturation.**

- RHD2216 is bipolar (EEG+ − EEG−, EMG+ − EMG−) but **still needs the animal body tied to headstage GND** so the animal's potential sits inside the amp input window. No tie = float = all channels rail together. Matches symptom exactly.
- The grounding bus grounds the **headstage/system**, not the **animal**. Bus↔GND-pad continuity was never the problem.
- **Fix:** dedicate a spare dura-contacting skull pin as animal ground → tether green wire → busbar → C3410 `GND`.

## Slip-ring COLOR-SWAP defect found — animal ground routes to nothing (new doc)

Source: operator PDF `Color mismatch.pdf` (current-status vs how-it-should-be). Full concepts write-up: [[grounding-reference-noise-concepts]].

**Wiring table (map by table, NOT colour — tether & slip ring use different colour conventions):**

| signal | tether | slip ring | C3410 pad |
|---|---|---|---|
| EEG frontal-L | red | red | 0 |
| EEG parietal-L | black | black | 1 |
| EMG L | yellow | yellow | 2 |
| EMG R | blue | blue | 3 |
| dura frontal-R | green | green | 4 |
| dura parietal-R | white | brown | 5 |
| Reference (copper coil) | peach | magenta | 6 |
| Ground bus | — | orange | gnd |

**Defect = single swap on the slip ring:** green↔orange, right column bottom two positions.
- should-be: …brown, blue, **orange, green**
- current: …brown, blue, **green, orange**
- ⇒ tether **green (tissue ground)** no longer mates slip-ring **orange (ground bus)** → animal ground lands on a dead line → **animal floats → railing returns.** One swap, but it's the ground = fatal (ground is physical, can't be fixed in software).
- white↔brown (pad5) and peach↔magenta (pad6) are NOT errors — just colour-convention differences; map by table.

**Slip ring is resin-potted with pins → NOT re-pinnable → must BUILD A NEW ONE** with orange (ground bus) aligned to tether green ("how it should be" layout). Current slip rings unusable. ⚠️ Don't compensate at the busbar/tether end — a later corrected slip ring would stack swaps and re-break ground.

**Copper tinned coil = environmental-noise reference, NOT ground, NOT brain ref.**
- It's the cable shield, **cut at the mouse end** → sees no tissue, only ambient EM along cable.
- Professor: still connect it to a tether-socket pin → **pad 6** = recorded env-noise channel (subtract in software later).
- Distinct from: animal ground (green→bus→GND) and brain reference (bipolar RHD2216 uses none → REF pad empty).

**Concept separation logged** (operator asked these be very clearly understood): ground (DC tie keeping animal in ±5mV window) ≠ reference (baseline to subtract) ≠ tissue noise (biological, from animal) ≠ environmental noise (external EM). "Reference" has two meanings: brain reference (biological, unused here/bipolar) vs environmental reference (copper coil). Full table in [[grounding-reference-noise-concepts]].

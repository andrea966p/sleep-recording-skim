## ROOT CAUSE FOUND — Setup B railing = animal has NO ground tie (not a cold joint)

Railing of the detected 2-mice headstage (10/12 ch at 10–12.6mV pk-pk, both mice railed together) is **NOT** the freshly-soldered GND-pad joint and **NOT** the busbar. Bench continuity proved the system-side ground chain is solid. The real cause: **no electrode carried headstage GND into the animal → animal floats → bipolar amp common-mode drives every channel into saturation.**

- RHD2216 is bipolar (EEG+ − EEG−, EMG+ − EMG−) but **still needs the animal body tied to headstage GND** so the animal's potential sits inside the amp input window. No tie = float = all channels rail together. Matches symptom exactly.
- The grounding bus grounds the **headstage/system**, not the **animal**. Bus↔GND-pad continuity was never the problem.
- **Fix:** dedicate a spare dura-contacting skull pin as animal ground → tether green wire → busbar → C3410 `GND`.

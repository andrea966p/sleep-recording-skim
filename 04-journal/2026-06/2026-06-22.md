## Grounding topology — two mice, one headstage (decided 2026-06-22)
Both animal grounds must land on the **same single ground net** — the RHD2216 has exactly one ground. Acceptable physical options (all electrically identical):
- Star both greens onto the C3410 `GND` pad, **OR**
- M1 green → C3410 `GND` pad, M2 green → **RHD2216 headstage `GND` pad** (same net, reached through the adapter↔headstage connector).

**Not independent grounds** — shared chip = shared ground always; this won't cure cross-talk if it appears (that needs a 2nd headstage). **`REF` pad stays empty** (bipolar RHD2216 → REF unused; it is NOT an alternate ground for M2). **Verify the two GND pads are common-net with a DMM continuity check (~0 Ω) before trusting it.**

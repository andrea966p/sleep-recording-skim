## Grounding topology — two mice, one headstage (decided)
> [!important] One shared ground net (RHD2216 has exactly one ground)
> Both animal grounds must land on the **same single GND net.** Acceptable, electrically-identical options:
> - star both greens onto the C3410 `GND` pad, **OR**
> - M1 green → C3410 `GND`, M2 green → RHD2216 headstage `GND` pad (same net via the adapter↔headstage connector).
>
> **Not independent grounds** — shared chip = shared ground always; this won't cure cross-talk if it appears (that needs a 2nd headstage). `REF` stays empty (bipolar). **DMM-verify the two GND pads are common-net (~0 Ω) before trusting it.**

*(This "shared chip = shared ground" decision is exactly what gets reopened on [[2026-06-30]] — where the "put each mouse on a separate RHD2216 chip for an independent ground" idea comes up.)*

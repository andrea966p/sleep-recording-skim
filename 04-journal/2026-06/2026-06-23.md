## Grounding — busbar star node decided (2026-06-23)
Method for moving both mice's grounds off the C3410 signal pads: use a **5-position brass grounding busbar** (all 5 screws = one common brass strip = one node) as the star hub.

```
M1 slip-ring GND ──► end screw 1 ┐
                                 ├─ common brass bar (star) ──► center screw ──► C3410 GND pad
M2 slip-ring GND ──► end screw 5 ┘
```

- Cleaner than soldering two thin wires to the tiny GND pad; screw-terminal, swappable, single wire to the pad.
- **Still ONE shared net** — busbar does not separate the mice (inherent to one headstage; won't cure cross-talk).
- Build: desolder greens from **pad 5 (M1)** + **pad 13 (M2)** → busbar; tin/fold thin wire tips so screws bite; center screw → C3410 GND pad; **DMM continuity screw1↔center↔screw5↔GND pad = ~0 Ω**; mount close to headstage (short leads); keep bar isolated.

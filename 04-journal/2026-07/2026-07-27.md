# 2026-07-27 — Monday — Video-EEG sync architecture finalized; the recording toggle root-caused

Council-reviewed day. Established that the Frigate recording toggle **can never deliver synchronisation**, root-caused a data-loss bug in Frigate's own source, and settled the alignment architecture.

## Built today

- ⭐ [[2026-07-27-video-eeg-sync-recording-architecture]] — **the architecture decision.** Constraints, root cause, the three reasons the toggle cannot sync, the rejected alternatives, and the final post-council decision.
- Updated [[gpio-sync-service]] and [[overview]] in the hardware-sync set.

## Headlines

- 🔴 **ROOT CAUSE CONFIRMED IN FRIGATE SOURCE: every toggled session permanently loses 10–20 s off its tail.** `maintainer.py` `drop_segment()` unlinks unflushed segments from `/tmp/cache` when recording is switched off. Accepted, not fixed.
- **The toggle can never be a sync mechanism**, for three independent reasons. Establishing that killed a whole class of proposed designs.
- ✅ **Alignment uses NO CLOCKS.** The pulse is emitted from the camera's **frame-capture callback** (using a timer would be circular), then `frame N ↔ sample S` is fitted, and the slope *is* the drift. Per-camera. **1 pulse / 10 s plus a double-width marker every 60 s.**
- **Auto-start WANTED. 24/7 continuous recording REJECTED** (455 GB). Frigate stays permanent.
- **Leading edge is free margin; only the STOP loses data.** That reframing simplified the whole design.
- **Fix specified for `daemon.py:447`:** drain 30 s, then release the lease, and **never publish OFF**. `enabled = (manual OR session)`, fail-ON at SIGTERM. This kills the retained-OFF failure class that cost a 72 h run.

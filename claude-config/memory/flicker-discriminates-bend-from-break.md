Measured on `allego_1__uid0901-12-38-53` (84 s, stable chip floors): EEG rms variability 80-150 % with max/min 7-55x = bending conductor (torsion, recoverable by handling); 4-14 % with max/min 1.1-1.8x = severed conductor (handling will NOT fix it); working channel 11-18 % (distinguish by lf/hf above its own floor p99). Two corroborating tells: both roles switching in lockstep = one cable bundle making and breaking; the other role AND the chip floor jumping in the SAME epoch = the tether body moved, never an electrode.

Details: [[tether-bend-vs-break-flicker]]

⚠️ A short file cannot rule out a SLOW intermittent (period of minutes): take 10 min before calling a break final. 🛑 The definitive test is mechanical, not statistical: record continuously while rotating and straightening ONE tether at a time. Closes when straight, opens when twisted => torsion proven, and the fix is a swivel or a shorter tether.

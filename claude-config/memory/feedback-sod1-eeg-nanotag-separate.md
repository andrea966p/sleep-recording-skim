When summarising SOD1 effects on sleep, keep the **EEG/EMG batches** (W-SD, S-SD, W-SL, S-SL) and the **nanotag batches** (HLBNSL, NS-SD/NW-SD) as separate findings. No "the nanotag agrees", no nanotag number used to downgrade or support an EEG hint, and no nanotag value as the plausibility reference for EEG staging. Andrea, 2026-10-06: "nanotag findings are separated from eeg findings for SOD effect on sleep".

**Why:** they are different methods (EEG/EMG staging vs immobility), different mice and different protocols. I had written "the nanotag batch agrees" into the EEG pages and used nanotag bout lengths to call an EEG consolidation hint unconfirmed.
**How to apply:** give one section per method, each with its own n and its own caveats. Plausibility checks for EEG come from EEG data or the literature. A project-level summary (the hub) may list both, side by side, never pooled. Related: [[sod1-sleep-eeg-2x2-2026-10-06]], [[nanotag-sod1-hlbnsl]].

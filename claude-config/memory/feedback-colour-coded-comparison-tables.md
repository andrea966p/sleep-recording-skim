Comparison and status tables (vendor answers vs our reads, settled vs open, options, superseded vs current) get a
first column of colour emoji plus a one-line `> [!info]` legend above the table:
🟢 agreed and settled · 🟡 uncertain / inferred, needs a test · 🟠 not answered, to ask · 🔴 not applicable to us /
superseded / ruled out · 🔵 our decision (Andrea / professor). Add other colours only with a legend entry.

**Why:** Andrea (2026-10-28) wants to see the state of each row at a glance for decisions and meetings with the professor.
**How to apply:** every new comparison table; when touching an older one, add the column. Adding the column to a record
note is not rewriting its claims. First applied to the Pinnacle notes (2026-10-25 to 10-28). See [[pinnacle-wireless-16-mice-evaluation]].

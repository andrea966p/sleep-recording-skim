In emails, **never use dashes** (`-` or `—`). Rephrase with commas, parentheses, colons, or separate sentences instead.

**Why:** dashes read as AI-generated and the user wants outgoing email to look human/clean.

**How to apply:** when drafting or editing any email body, strip em-dashes and hyphen-as-punctuation; recast compound phrases so no dash is needed. Extends the general [[feedback_report_writing_style]] "commas-not-dashes" rule, but for EMAILS it is absolute.

### B1: a floating chip, and the comparison that settled the port question

B1's floor 60 Hz by hour, all 24 bins: 91, 184, 307, 189, 72, 200, 245, 170, 466, 379, 266, 203,
341, 299, 139, 258, 302, 235, 462, 458, 214, 249, 257, 235, 184 µV. **No jump anywhere.** It was
floating when the recording started and floating when it stopped.

The decisive comparison is the two slots of the **same port**:

```
B slot 1: floor 60 Hz   246 uV    <- floating
B slot 2: floor 60 Hz  4896 uV    <- normal
```

Same port, same cable, same XDAQ input, opposite results ⇒ **port B, its cable and its XDAQ input
are innocent.** The fault is in the slot-1 branch only. At the time I read that as the ground pin
in the connector housing and called B1 a standing port fault across four cohorts.

> [!danger] Retracted
> On 2026-08-26 the B1 animal HLBCSD13 was pulled **because its tether was chewed**, and the spare
> HLBCSD17 was plugged into the **same slot**. The floor went **246 → 2186 µV**, grounded, with real
> EEG and real EMG. **The fault followed the animal and its tether.** The measurements above stand;
> the causal claim does not. 🔑 **A recurrence argument is not a swap test.**

Animals are active in the dark and movement flips the contact. 🔑 **Dark-phase EEG yield is
materially lower, and the dark phase is where most WAKE lives, so a 24 h run does not give 24 h of
scoreable data and the loss is biased toward one behavioural state.** Any sleep architecture
computed from a partially usable 24 h record inherits that bias, which is one more reason rung 4
stays PROVISIONAL until the kappa exists.

⚠️ **A2 degrades progressively over the last 5 h** — hourly EEG usable for clock hours 04 to 08:
14, 10, 21, 38, 42%, against 90 to 100% for the whole light phase. That was the first sign of the
failure that completed on 26 August, when A2's EEG rms went to 3856 µV.

## Still open, from this run

1. 🟡 The dark-phase yield problem is a **design** problem, not a bug. Either the contact has to be
   made robust to movement, or long runs have to be planned around a materially lower dark yield.
2. 🟡 A2's five-hour decline was visible in this file and was not acted on. A per-hour usable-%
   trace should be looked at on every long run, not just the total.
3. The blind-scoring **kappa** is still the gate.

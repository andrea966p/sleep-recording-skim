# 2026-11-02: NOR validated, tissue assays analysed, final report draft v0.1

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> - **NOR (HLBNSL):** forepaw rule adopted, pose scoring validated against two blinded raters (frame kappa 0.76). No
>   SOD1 effect on d2; 9/9 mice explore the novel object more, confounded with the blue prism.

## NOR hand validation (HLBNSL)
- Forepaw rule reviewed against the literature and adopted (Andrea): sniffing with the forepaws on the object counts,
  no `paws_on` key. SOP section 3 and `nor_explore.py` revised, batch 1 re-scored (d2 changed by 0.02 at most).
  [[2026-11-02-nor-forepaw-rule-literature-review]]. Andrea's clips NOR_247 and NOR_685 already followed this rule.
- Andrea scored NOR_247, 685, 899, 936. Hand vs pose: totals r 0.94, ICC 0.80 (model1) / 0.69 (model2), models
  4-6 s less per object; hand key presses lag about 0.5 s. [[2026-10-26-HLBNSL-nor-hand-scoring#Validation result (2026-11-02, 4 clips, revised forepaw rule)]]
- Rater 2 scored NOR_238 + NOR_554 (calibration test, `nor_calibrate.py`) and the overlap clips NOR_247 + NOR_685:
  frame kappa 0.76, ICC 0.93, rater 2 +3.3 s per object. Pooled novel preference 9/9 mice (model1 p = 0.004),
  confounded with the blue prism and its side. [[2026-11-02-HLBNSL-nor-hand-validation-and-calibration]]

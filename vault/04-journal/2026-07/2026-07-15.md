### What they agreed on (5 of 5)

- **No scoreable sleep data exists yet, across ANY recording.** The only genuinely physiological trace in the whole dataset is one roughly 42 second clip (A2 of `allego_0__uid0714-16-58-16`, delta-dominant real cortical EEG), far too short to stage sleep. Everything else is unusable.
- **The hum is DIFFERENTIAL at the amplifier, so a notch plus bipolar CANNOT rescue it.** The metrologist proved the buried biology is not just hidden, it is ABSENT, the connected mouse A2 on 07-15 is spectrally identical to an open wire-to-nowhere channel.
- **The floating slots B, C, D are NOT a valid "earth control".** Open unconnected inputs pick up 60 Hz no matter what the system earth is doing, so their loudness tells you nothing about the earth. This was an error in my own evidence packet, all five caught it independently.
- **Per-electrode impedance was never measured before the 07-15 runs, and it is the cheapest thing that would settle the argument.** (I ran it, see section 4.)

### The root cause the council converged on

It is BOTH earth and contact, and the two "camps" were measuring two factors of one product, not disagreeing:

```
differential_60Hz  ~  common_mode_mains(earth floor)  x  electrode_impedance_imbalance(contact)
```

- The common-mode mains field is REAL and present, one single source drives every channel (phase-locking value 1.00 across all slots), and a connected electrode sat at roughly 3 mV of single-ended 60 Hz, which a solidly-earthed electrode physically cannot do. So the system is still effectively floating, that sets the FLOOR.
- But the montage does not reject that field, because electrode-impedance imbalance converts it to a differential signal (bipolar-to-common-mode 60 Hz ratio about 2). The electrode contact is the BINDING, controllable variable, same earth, same instant, one channel clean and another dirty depending only on its contact.

The honest disagreement was the relative weight of earth versus contact, and one advisor (the contrarian) raised a hypothesis nobody else did and that I had not considered, that the electrician's new permanent wall-earth bond may have created a GROUND LOOP, which would explain why 07-15 is actually WORSE than the pre-fix floating baseline. That is a live minority possibility, roughly 20%, and a single earth-lift test settles it. The display, RustDesk, and analysis-artifact hypotheses were rejected, the hum was present before RustDesk even connected.

### What the council forced me to retract (this is the no-bias payoff)

The council caught my OWN vault overstating things, and I corrected them:

- "Earthing fix VALIDATED / paper-ready 2026-07-14" was OVERSTATED. It rested on one 42 second single-electrode clip, with the other electrode dirty in the same instant, and that electrode swung bad within minutes. That proves a matched electrode rejects common-mode, it does NOT prove the earth fix works system-wide. Correction callout added to [[2026-07-14]].
- The "matched before/after pair" was NOT a controlled comparison, two variables changed at once.
- The QC metric `60Hz%raw` is denominator-confounded and should not be the primary number, use absolute microvolts instead. **This is Intan's own recommendation, not a hack.** Their Noise Reduction Techniques application note (10 Dec 2024) states "a cardboard box covered in aluminum foil is equally effective at stopping electric fields as an expensive wire-mesh cage" and explains the mechanism, mains wiring couples into the amplifier input through a small capacitance C1 forming a capacitive voltage divider, and the Faraday cage "is tied to ground, and this provides a new termination point for C1, so the interfering signal never reaches the amplifier."

**Rules we are probably still breaking** (from the same note):
- The shield MUST be tied to a ground point OUTSIDE the cage. Ours looks like floating foil, so we are likely leaving most of the ~40 dB on the table.
- "It should not be electrically connected to anything inside the cage." The animal and headstage GND must stay isolated from the foil, "try to keep your tissue electrically isolated from the Faraday cage."
- Cover all sides. Ours is partial.

### 7d. Literature check, is our long analog tether actually wrong? NO.
I claimed the lid-mounted headstage with a long analog run "defeats the Intan design". **That was too strong and I withdraw it.** Sources:

## 8. 🎉 THE BREAKTHROUGH — first sleep biology ever recorded, and every archive re-analyzed

### 8a. Andrea's methodological challenge (which was RIGHT)
Andrea pushed back: "are you sure you are analyzing the EEG correctly and not simply averaging out everything? If you don't filter correctly then of course you will see noise if you average. If you look at the data in epochs, maybe it will be clean." **He was right, and it changed the verdict.** Two real flaws in my QC:
1. **`phys%` used a denominator running to Nyquist (500 Hz)**, so high-frequency junk above 40 Hz tanked the score even when the EEG band was clean. A scorer NEVER looks at 200 Hz, they bandpass it away. My metric was unfairly harsh.
2. **I concatenated windows then ran Welch** — the same smearing bug the council found in `multimouse_scan.py`. Non-stationary hum smears across the average.

Redone scorer-style (bipolar, 60 Hz comb, bandpass 0.5-30 Hz EEG / 10-100 Hz EMG, metrics PER 4 s EPOCH), four slots of the 45 s clip jumped to physiological amplitude (32-88 µV) with delta-dominant spectra and zero clipping. Completely different from "0 of 7 unusable."

### 8b. But amplitude is not proof — the state-structure test
"Delta-dominant at the right amplitude" is NECESSARY, NOT SUFFICIENT, a quiet or dead high-impedance input bandpassed to 0.5-30 Hz also shows low-amplitude 1/f that LOOKS delta-dominant. **The only thing separating real brain from a well-shielded noise floor is structure over time**, delta/theta cycling, EMG dropping in sleep, states persisting in bouts. So Andrea ran a **164-minute** recording (`allego_0__uid0715-18-58-54`, byte-perfect).

**A1 showed textbook sleep architecture.** EMG high (~1000 µV, active) for 0.2 h, then **dropped to ~250 µV and stayed there a solid hour** (0.25-1.2 h), then jumped back to ~800-1000 µV with bouts. During that quiet hour **theta/delta sat at ~0.2**, delta dominating theta 5 to 1. Then theta/delta rose past 1 in bouts. That is: mouse handled, settles, NREM sleep for an hour, wakes with bouts. The delta-versus-EMG scatter is **bimodal, two distinct clusters**. A noise floor cannot do that.

> [!warning] A metric of mine that MISLED, documented so nobody repeats it. I predicted delta and EMG should ANTI-correlate and got +0.49 / +0.21 / +0.30, the "wrong" sign, and nearly called the data dead because of it. The flaw is mine, not the data's: **motion artifact during wake dumps broadband low-frequency power into the delta band**, inflating absolute delta exactly when EMG is high, so the raw correlation goes positive even when the state structure is real. The honest readouts are the **theta/delta RATIO** and the **EMG time course**.

### 8c. The heuristic I wrote FIRST was a false-positive generator (corrected)
My initial verdict rule was "EMG 10-90 percentile range > 3x and theta/delta range > 3x = STATE STRUCTURE". **That tests VARIANCE, not STRUCTURE — any noisy signal passes.** It labelled 6 of 8 slots of the (dead) 72 h run as "STATE STRUCTURE". Replaced with SLEEP-SPECIFIC tests in `state_scan.py`:
- **`td_sep`** (THE gate) = Cliff's delta of theta/delta between LOW-EMG and HIGH-EMG epochs. Real sleep: low EMG = NREM = delta-rich = LOW theta/delta → **strongly NEGATIVE**. Noise has no reason to produce this. Threshold < -0.25.
- `bimod` = bimodality coefficient of log EMG (two states). `persist` = EMG autocorrelation at 5 min (sleep bouts last minutes). `circ` = fraction of log-EMG spectral power at 20-28 h period; **a real mouse over ≥24 h MUST show a day/night rhythm**.
- Layout **derived from JSON `sensors_by_port`**, never hardcoded (fixes the mis-mapping bug from §6).

**Why the gate matters:** the dead 72 h run scores `persist +0.93` and `bimod 0.80` — it LOOKS structured by those measures (that's slow drift and bimodal noise). Only `td_sep` separates it.

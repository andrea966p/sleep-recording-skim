## Focus

Investigate Diane's report of "all videos fired at once 4h ago." Result: third recurrence of the [[2026-04-01|2026-04-01]] Docker-upgrade pattern, plus a new daemon startup-guard regression. Apply trigger-level fix, clean up unwanted data, then plan the stabilization sprint that picks up where the [[../../07-decisions/2026-05-01-llm-council-review|May 1 council]] and [[2026-05-18|2026-05-18 watchdog work]] left off.

## TL;DR

- **11:46 today** — GNOME Update Manager (via `aptdaemon`, bypassing `50unattended-upgrades` blacklist) upgraded `docker-ce 29.5.0 → 29.5.1`. Docker restarted Frigate, which re-enabled recording from `record.enabled: true` in its config. All 4 cameras recorded for **4h 25min** before Diane noticed and stopped them at 16:11.
- **16:28** — during the cleanup, a manual `docker start frigate` triggered the same auto-record + four spurious `ManualSession` records with new IDs. The daemon's documented 10s startup grace period guards **daemon** startup, not **Frigate** startup with the daemon already running — newly identified gap.
- **Trigger fix applied:** `sudo apt-mark hold docker-ce docker-ce-cli docker-ce-rootless-extras docker-buildx-plugin docker-compose-plugin containerd.io` — the only mechanism `aptdaemon` respects.
- **Cleanup:** ~13 GB of unwanted recordings, exports, and session JSONs purged.
- **Diane re-started her recording at 16:37–16:38** (pi_cam_0/1/2). Recording runs until Friday lunchtime — **no system touches** until then.
- Full incident detail in [[2026-05-20-docker-upgrade-and-startup-guard-regression]].

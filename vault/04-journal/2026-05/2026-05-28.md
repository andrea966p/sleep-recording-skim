## Why suppress `orphan`

Frigate is configured with `record.enabled: true`, so it writes continuous 24/7 segments on all 4 cameras independent of any session. The reconciler classifies any continuous segment run not covered by a WAL `START` as `ORPHAN`. That means every 24 h window will contain ~4 baseline orphans (one per camera) even on a perfectly healthy system. Surfacing those to Slack would be unactionable noise; only `partial / stuck / degraded` reflect real anomalies. This matches the rationale documented in [[../../06-references/pattern_frigate_record_enabled_orphan_noise|orphan-noise pattern]] and the reconciler design.

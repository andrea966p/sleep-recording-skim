**C1's event is even more diagnostic.** At epoch 17 its EEG, its EMG AND its chip floor all jump in

🔴 **B1, B2 and D1 are NOT torsion.** They hold a fixed open level for 84 seconds — 4.0%, 4.3% and

⭐ Worth keeping: **flicker statistics discriminate a bend from a break, and they work on a file far

# 2026-09-26 — S-SL gets sleep data, and a published conclusion gets retracted

## What happened, shortest version

Ran stage 2 and stage 3 on the S-SL day folders for the first time. The results
contradicted the verdict ladder so hard that they exposed a QC defect, which in
turn invalidated a conclusion pushed earlier the same day.

  09-25 and 09-26 for both jobs — 77 stripped files (68.1 GB), 84 raw (1018.2 GB).
  The 09-24 run did the work; since then both converge in ~50 s.
- **`W-SD.yaml` per-animal rows.** 53 rows across 8 animals, generated from the
  manifest's own placements so they assert nothing the resolver did not already
  conclude. The three UNIDENTIFIED B1 rows are correct — that animal chewed its
  tether and was never named.
  - ⚠️ Correction: these do **not** fix an unset `role`. It was already `"animal"`,
    the default in `RecordingRef.from_obj`. What they buy is the card timeline and
    the `validate()` cross-check.
- **`allego_0__uid0920-09-00-07` found.** 15.145 h of real S-SL data in no
  manifest, carded only under a throwaway cohort. It is the Sunday first segment
  of the recovery run: ends 09-21 00:08:48, and `uid0921` starts 8 min 46 s later.
  Allego split one run when it was restarted.
- **All seven `auto-*` console cohorts retired** from `cards/out/`. One,
  `auto-6255ffcf2575`, was not a duplicate but a **stale** `uid0917` set reaching

Never run before on this batch. Rig idle, 654 GB free.

⭐ **S-SL has a paired n = 3 at rung 3**: HLBSSL31 (A1), HLBSSL33 (B1),
HLBSSL35 (C1), scoreable at both protocol timepoints. Better than anything
previously claimed for this batch.

🛑 **No sleep conclusion drawn.** Baseline is 12.0 h light / 12.06 h dark;
endpoint is 16.0 / 20.71 — dark-heavy because it starts at 00:17. Mice are more
awake in the dark, so the whole-file "more wake at recovery" is mostly window
composition. In the light phase all three move the same way by +1.4 to +4.1
points; in the dark the direction is inconsistent. Suggestive at most, n = 3.

🔑 This is the **first concrete answer to the trimmed-window question**. Trimming
the endpoint to its protocol window drops exactly the 8.71 h of dark that skews it.

## The defect, and the retraction

Stage 3 scored six animals that rung 2 called disconnected. Chasing that:

**Every S-SL EEG channel is unchanged between baseline and endpoint** — slope
≈ −1.55, rms ≈ 70 µV. Only the chips' reference floors moved, on five of eight,
to −2.4…−3.5. D2 was genuinely dead at baseline (−0.076, 29.6 µV) and is healthy
at the endpoint (−1.644, 78.0 µV) — it recovered, and rung 2 calls it 0.0%.

Falsification: a filter change would move every chip together. B1 and C2 did not
move, and they are exactly the two that "passed".

🛑 **Retracted commit `5ccdd55`**, pushed this morning: "the collapse happened
before the recovery run, not during it". No collapse happened at all.

🔴 **The defect was already known.** It was diagnosed completely on
**2026-08-27** in [[05-experiments/completed/2026-08-27-B1-swap-baseline-20h-run]]
— mechanism, correct verdict (UNJUDGEABLE), missing guard, all of it — and left
as an open item on one experiment page. A month later it cost a published
conclusion. **That is the actual lesson of today**, more than the bug itself.

## The scan

Read-only over every card JSON plus cached features. **11 of 79 slope-judged
cards affected**, across S-SL, W-SL and S-SD.

- First pass said 35. Wrong: 16 were judged on **hum**, where the slope floor is
  unused, and several flagged FAILs are real open electrodes.
- Three flags are **genuine** — `uid0825` D1, `uid0826` D1, `uid0819-17-25-20` B1
  — all flat slope *with* railing amplitude (2080–2759 µV). The last is the
  chewed tether.
- ✅ **W-SD's 39.7 h pre-CSD baseline is clean.** The project's best full-cohort
  data is unaffected.
- ✅ **No false PASSes.** Zero cards pass on an over-flat floor. The error is
  one-directional: it only ever rejects good data.

Full detail: [[03-troubleshooting/symptoms/contaminated-floor-rejects-good-eeg]].

## Evening: the floor fix

Closes open items 1–3.

- **`floor_stats()` fixed.** Commits `a52133c` and `2cc5892`. A grounded chip whose
  slope threshold is steeper than −1.90 gives UNJUDGEABLE, not FAIL.
- 🔴 **Rejected the 08-27 detector after measuring it.** It compares the floor with the
  animal, and a dead electrode is as flat as the floor, so it flagged all three genuinely
  open electrodes (1.00 / 1.00 / 0.93).
- 🔴 **My first fix was falsified within the hour.** It tested the floor's *typical* site at
  −0.85, fitted on 12 cached recordings. The first unfitted recording, `uid0920` C1, had a
  typical site of −0.77 and a threshold of −3.00. The contamination was intermittent and sat
  in the tail. Stopped and asked, rather than nudging the cutoff to fit.
- **Re-carded four recordings only.** Exactly the scan's 11 cards moved; no PASS moved;
  the genuine failures stand. Cards stamped `2cc5892`.
- **W-SL n is still 1.** D2 fails scoreability at BASELINE (r 0.174), so no 09-17 verdict
  could pair it. The banner's worry had the wrong premise.
- ⚠️ **S-SL's paired n = 3 is a stage 3 claim.** The ladder now reads A1 and C1 at the
  endpoint as UNJUDGEABLE: no longer rejected, but not confirmed either.

Still open: the `check_hum_validity()` route; re-carding `uid0912` (it predates stage 3, so
rung 3 reads INSUFFICIENT); items 4 and 5 above.

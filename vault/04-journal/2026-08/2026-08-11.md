### Also visible, and all three are what a mouse should do

1. Sleep pressure decays across the light phase, 76 percent asleep at ZT1 down to 49 at ZT10.
2. Sharp transition at lights off: ZT12 45 percent, ZT13 14 percent, EMG 47 → 77 µV.
3. ⭐ A mid-dark rest bout at **ZT19-20**, and it shows on **both channels at once**, delta up and
   EMG down together. Two independent measures moving together is a behavioural state, not a
   scoring artefact.

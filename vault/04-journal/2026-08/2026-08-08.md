## The council

Five advisors, then anonymised peer review. Full findings in §1 of the review page. The ones that
mattered:

- 🔴 **Selection bias:** the QC gate rejects on contact, contact is movement, movement is state.
- 🔴 **4 of 5 physiology checks cannot fail** — I verified in code; the council said 3, it is worse.
- 🔴 **"Threshold-independent" is false** — Otsu is refit per animal.
- 🔴 **The wire-free nulls are pseudoreplicated.**
- 🔴 **Accuracy is unmeasured** — no scorer has ever run.
- ⛔ **They rejected the premise of my own question.** "Modernise the staging" is wrong at n = 2
  with no ground truth; sophisticated staging on unverified channels **hides** the failure mode we
  spent three weeks learning to see.

## Three results worth keeping separate from the fixes

> [!danger] 🔴 The circadian validation is threshold-dependent
> ±25% on one per-animal threshold moves D2 from **23.0% to 88.9%** asleep in the light phase.
> The ~70% literature value is reachable from almost anywhere in that range.
> **It is not the independent confirmation it was presented as.**
> ⚠️ I had called D2's 70.3% "the cleaner number" because it sat nearer the literature mean.
> **Wrong** — D2 is the *most* fragile animal, 65.9 points against B1's 35.9.

> [!success] ✅ The selection bias is not operating
> Two of three scoreable recordings rejected **zero** epochs, so no selection could have occurred.
> The third rejected 1.3%, and those epochs carry **lower** EMG — the opposite direction to the
> movement-driven bias predicted. Correct concern, does not apply to this dataset.

> [!success] ⭐ REM order is real
> Permuting the ORDER of state runs within blocks, keeping lengths and composition, gives a null of
> 56.6% ± 1.6%. Observed is **72.4%**, and the other two recordings are 4.8 and 11.6 SD out.
> The council suspected this was forced by composition. **It is not**, and it is now the strongest
> informative check we have.

## The Japanese tool's source code

We were given `SleepyScoreApp_HM_260312_exported.m` and `sleepy_pipeline_refined.py`. Both implement
the same algorithm, so it could be read off rather than inferred.
Full comparison: [[sleepyscore-japan-scoring-tool-algorithm]].

⭐⭐ **They do not use a percentile for REM.** Both thresholds are **2-cluster k-means splits**,
WAKE on the EMG rectified integral and REM on theta/delta among sleep epochs. **That is exactly the
fix for our p80 rule**, which forces REM to 20% by construction.

⚠️ It also contradicted a change I had made hours earlier: I set delta to 1-4 Hz from the paper; the
tool low-passes at **4.5 Hz**. Both right, different jobs — paper = reporting, tool = staging. Now
two constants.

🛑 **Nothing from the tool was applied.** Swapping the REM rule changes every REM number, and the
council was explicit that method changes at n = 2 with no ground truth are unfalsifiable.
**Order: score the packs → kappa → then swap and measure whether kappa improves.**

## Bugs found and fixed today

- **Minimum-bout merge capped at 100 iterations** per block, far too few for 21,000 epochs. Only
  115 epochs merged and short bouts survived. Corrected to `len(seg)`; 2,083 merged.
- **`nm` shadowed** by two later loop variables, crashing the REM null with a `TypeError`.
- **`KeyError: emg_otsu_log10`** on all 13 rejected animals, because `stage()` returns an empty
  threshold dict when nothing is usable. Guarded, then given the median fallback.
- **"State totals both ways" was degenerate** as first written: blocks contain only passing epochs,
  so "% of epochs in blocks" can never differ from "% of scored epochs". Replaced with the
  whole-recording denominator.

### 🔴🔴 THE HUM CRITERION IS DEAD, AND A1 KILLED IT

**A1 has no ground and scores 100.0 / 74.6 / 100.0 percent "usable" by the legacy hum criterion.**
With no ground, mains drives the animal as common mode and a bipolar pair rejects it, so a
floating channel is **quiet**: A1's median 60 Hz is 44 to 78 µV against an unwired floor median of
**3962 µV**. The criterion rewards quiet, so it hands a floating animal a perfect score.
⇒ **`qc/usable_fraction_vs_floor.py` MUST NOT be run on this cohort.** New script
**`qc/usable_fraction_vs_floor_ecg.py`** reports both criteria; **use the SLOPE column**, which
tests 1 to 40 Hz where no mains harmonic lives, so it needs no notch and no filter design.
⚠️ **Hum-criterion and slope-criterion usable fractions are NOT comparable.** Never table them
together with B1's 99.6 percent from 2026-08-06/07.

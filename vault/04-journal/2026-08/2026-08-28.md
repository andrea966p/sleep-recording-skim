Both had floating EEG for the whole 20 h — 55 and 67 µV, slope about −0.1, lf/hf well under their
own floors — while their **EMG worked continuously**, 98.4% and 99.2%, and A1's muscle amplitude is
**2.73× higher in the dark**, which is genuine state dependence. Muscle signal arriving through the
same tether while the EEG floats puts the fault on the EEG side of the chain, not in the implant.

That also retires the caution I wrote yesterday, that a quiet "EMG 100%" might itself be a floating
input. On these two it is real.

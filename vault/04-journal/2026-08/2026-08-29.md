## 🔴 The code called this a FAIL and should have refused

`FLOOR_MIN_HUM_UV = 800`, so A1 at 1347, A2 at 1575 and D1 at 1814 all cleared the grounded gate.
Across the 32 chip-recordings on disk, **six chips below 2000 µV, none produced EEG**; the lowest
floor that ever produced EEG is **2186 µV**. No overlap anywhere.

Raising it to 2000 moves A1, A2 and D1 on 08-27 and B1 on 08-24 from FAIL to UNJUDGEABLE. No PASS is
affected. **I have not changed the constant** — it rewrites verdicts in three published reports, so
it needs a decision rather than a commit. Evidence table is in
[[weak-chip-ground-vs-open-electrode]].

The difference is not bookkeeping. FAIL reads as "this animal's electrode is bad" and points at a
tether change under isoflurane. UNJUDGEABLE reads as "the rig could not answer" and points at a
connector re-mate at the bench, which is what actually fixed A1 and A2.

## Correction: what the rebound recording actually is

Andrea pinned the window down: **13:00 Thursday to 09:00 Friday**, which is
`allego_2__uid0827-12-58-32`, Thursday 2026-08-27 12:58:30 → Friday 2026-08-28 09:05:45, 20.121 h.
**That is the file I analysed, so every number in the reports stands.**

What I had wrong was the framing. I wrote it up as the endpoint *after* seven days of deprivation,
as though the protocol had finished. **It had not.** CSD ran from Friday 2026-08-21 to sampling on
Friday 2026-08-28, with each day's deprivation bout ending at 13:00, so **Thursday 08-27 was the
second to last day and the protocol was still running across the recording window.** Seven days had
*elapsed*; that is not the same as CSD being over.

Corrected in [[2026-08-27-W-SD-recovery-sleep]], [[2026-08-28-W-SD-cohort-closeout]],
[[pre-flight-connection-screen]], [[2026-08-27]], `_INDEX.md` and `cohorts/W-SD.yaml`. The title of
the run report is now "Recovery sleep on the penultimate SD day, Thursday 13:00 to Friday 09:00".

🔑 **Worth keeping: "after N days of CSD" is ambiguous and I read it the wrong way.** Write the
window, the weekday and the protocol state instead — *recovery window following the penultimate
deprivation bout, protocol still running* — because whether deprivation was finished changes what
the rebound means physiologically, even though it changes none of the connection numbers.

🔑 **The trap in that file, and I fell into it first.** Its whole-run medians say A1 slope −0.49 and
33.3% usable, which reads as a relapse. It is not. At minute 50 **EEG and EMG go open together on
both port-A positions and the chip floors step down with them** — the animals were unplugged for
sampling. A signal fault cannot take two roles on two chips in the same instant, and it cannot move
the floor. **Always check whether a channel died or the animal left**, and the tell is the floor plus
the other role, not the channel on its own.

101-153 µV with a steep slope, including B1 and C1 whose chips have no ground at all. Real EEG cannot
exist on an ungrounded chip, so that is drifting unterminated inputs. Not this project's; flagged in
both pages so it is never adopted into a cohort.

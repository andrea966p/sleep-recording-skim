## 1. ROOT CAUSE — animal has no ground tie (not a cold joint) ✅

> [!important] The unlock
> The railing (10/12 ch at 10–12.6 mV, both mice together) is **NOT** the freshly-soldered `GND`-pad joint and **NOT** the busbar. **No electrode carried headstage GND into the animal → the animal floats → the bipolar amp's common-mode drives every channel into saturation.**
> RHD2216 is bipolar but **still needs the animal body tied to headstage GND** so the animal's potential sits inside the amp window. The bus grounded the *system*, not the *animal*. **Fix:** dedicate a dura-contacting skull pin as animal ground → green → busbar → C3410 `GND`.

**Electrode / tether map (single-mouse implant):** custom 4-pin skull electrode, all 4 pins on dura. Frontal + caudal-left → red/black = EEG; blue/yellow = EMG; white/green = the 2 previously-unused skull pins. **Tether green → right caudal dura pin = ANIMAL GROUND** (the railing fix). `REF` empty (bipolar).

## 2. Bench tests (off-site, no XDAQ)

**Setup B ground chain — ALL GOOD** (beep mode): C3410 `GND` pad ↔ soldered socket pin ✅; `GND` pad ↔ chip GND ✅; wiggle test stable ✅; full tether/slip-ring/busbar path beeps end-to-end. → **no cold joint.** The missing piece was always the animal-side tie.

**Setup A undetected headstage — no bench fault found:** VDD↔GND resistance = normal RC cap ramp (no short), SPI Omnetics top-row neighbours no short, pins straight/clean. No smoking gun → consistent with an SPI **signal-pin contact** fault only visible under clocking. **Decisive test = socket-swap at XDAQ** (still pending → carries to [[2026-06-29]]).

> [!note] Slip ring = noise suspect, NOT rail suspect
> Rotary contacts beep fine but add resistance + movement/50 Hz artifact on analog lines. They won't *rail* (rail = open). And they're downstream of the RHD chip → irrelevant to Setup A's SPI enumeration.

## 3. RESULT (evening, at building) — GROUNDING FIX CONFIRMED ✅

Connected tether green → right caudal dura pin (animal ground), streamed live single mouse, read raw metrics.

**Railing collapsed → animal is grounded.** Before: ~all 12 ch pegged at 10–12.6 mV (floating). After: noise floor exists (ch 20–31 ~35 µV RMS) and a channel is genuinely clean:
- **native ch1 (pad 1, D/2): RMS 30 µV, pk-pk 178 µV, SNR 11.7** = clean electrode → proof a good channel is reachable.

**Caveats (RAW, unfiltered):** many channels still float/rail — but these are **unwired inputs** (single mouse = only 4 electrodes wired; the rest float to rail on bipolar RHD). Not bad electrodes, just unwired. Hum dominates until filtered.

> [!success] Bottom line
> The **tissue (animal) ground was the unlock.** The hard hardware problem (floating animal / railing) is SOLVED. ch1 SNR 11.7 confirms clean signal is achievable; once colours are matched + filters applied, the signal is workable.

## 4. Slip-ring COLOUR-SWAP defect — animal ground routes to nothing 🔴

Source: operator PDF `Color mismatch.pdf`. Full concepts: [[grounding-reference-noise-concepts]]. **Map by the table, NOT by colour** (tether & slip ring use different colour conventions):

| signal | tether | slip ring | C3410 pad |
|---|---|---|---|
| EEG frontal-L | red | red | 0 |
| EEG parietal-L | black | black | 1 |
| EMG L | yellow | yellow | 2 |
| EMG R | blue | blue | 3 |
| dura frontal-R | green | green | 4 |
| dura parietal-R | white | brown | 5 |
| Reference (copper coil) | peach | magenta | 6 |
| Ground bus | — | orange | gnd |

**Defect = a single slip-ring swap: green↔orange.** Tether **green (tissue ground)** no longer mates slip-ring **orange (ground bus)** → animal ground lands on a dead line → animal floats → railing returns. One swap, but it's the ground = fatal. (white↔brown and peach↔magenta are just colour-convention differences, not errors.)

The slip ring is **resin-potted → not re-pinnable → must build a NEW one** (orange aligned to tether green). ⚠️ Don't compensate at the busbar/tether end — a later corrected slip ring would stack swaps. *(Definitive rebuild map → [[2026-06-28]].)*

> [!important] Four concepts, kept separate (operator asked these be crystal-clear)
> **Ground** (DC tie keeping the animal in the ±5 mV window; green → bus → `GND`) ≠ **reference** (a baseline to subtract) ≠ **tissue noise** (biological) ≠ **environmental noise** (external EM). And "reference" is two things: **brain reference** (biological, unused — bipolar RHD2216 → `REF` empty) vs **environmental reference** (the copper coil → pad 6). The copper coil = cable shield, **cut at the mouse** → sees only ambient EM, NOT ground, NOT a brain ref. Full table: [[grounding-reference-noise-concepts]].

## 6. XDAT reader VERIFIED on a real recording ✅

Recorded a 6 s test set via the SDK → `/home/skimlab/radix/data/allego_0__uid0627-21-11-02*` (kept as a known-good format sample), read it back, corrected the guide.

**Verified XDAT format (radiens 4.0.0):**
- 3-file set: `_data.xdat` + `_timestamp.xdat` + `.xdat.json`.
- `_data.xdat` = **float32**, interleaved sample-major; size = `num_smpl × total × 4`. **NOT int16, no 0.195 µV scaling** (earlier guess wrong). Primary channels **already in µV** (`sig_units_pri`).
- `total = 38` = `pri 32 + aux 2 + din 2 + dout 2`.
- Meta under `status`: `samp_freq`, `num_smpl`, `signals.*`. `_timestamp.xdat` = int64.

**Pipeline verified on the real file:** load → decimate 30 k→250 Hz → EEG bandpass 0.5–30 + 50 Hz notch → ch1 RMS 24.7 µV (matches the live SNR 11.7).

> [!warning] Environment gotcha (the recurring one)
> The project venv has NumPy 2.x but **no matching SciPy** → `import scipy.signal` crashes. The numpy-only XDAT reader runs fine there; the SciPy pipeline needs a **dedicated analysis venv** (numpy 2.5.0 + scipy 1.18.0). *(This is exactly the clean venv built on [[2026-06-30]] → [[_eeg-analysis-hub]].)* Also: SDK offline readers need the Videre hub (headless fails) → direct file read preferred; `set_recording_config()` has a serialization bug in 4.0.0 (use the GUI).

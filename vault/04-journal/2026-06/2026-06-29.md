> [!note] Why the EMG is the implant, not the rig
> Computed the EMG bipolar (2−3) from the raw stream: **RMS 2466 µV, does NOT cancel.** If both EMG tips sat in the same muscle they'd share common-mode and 2−3 would shrink to tens of µV. Instead each pad swings rail-to-rail independently (12–19 % of samples pinned at the ±6.4 mV rail) = both inputs open. The impedance (~7 MΩ each) confirms it. Connectors/slip-ring/ground are all good — the EEG on the same path is clean.

**Diagnosis:** EEG montage validated (pads 0,1 → bipolar 0−1 = the sleep EEG). **Both EMG electrodes open at the muscle end** → fix is surgical (redo the EMG wires). **This mouse is EEG-only** — EEG alone can't cleanly split REM from quiet wake (EMG atonia is the discriminator). Montage matches the [[tsukuba-sleep-recording-method|IIIS Tsukuba method]].

## 4. ⚠️ Incident — SDK impedance RPC crashed allegoserver

> [!warning] Never call `GetIntanImpedance` while streaming
> The SDK has **no impedance-measure RPC** — only `GetIntanImpedance`, a getter for the last GUI sweep, and Intan impedance needs acquisition **paused** (it injects a 1 kHz test current). Calling it mid-stream via raw gRPC returned `Socket closed` and **crashed allegoserver** (port 50051 refused — the known crash, see [[signal-mapping-guide]] §10).
> **Recovery:** restart Allego → re-set mode `XDAQ_ONE_REC` → re-set sample rate 1000 → re-select channels (the daemon auto re-pairs). **Measure impedance only from the GUI Impedance module.**

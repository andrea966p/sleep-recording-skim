## Thread 2 — Grounding busbar star node decided ✅

Method for moving both mice's grounds off the C3410 signal pads:

```
M1 slip-ring GND ──► end screw 1 ┐
                                 ├─ common brass bar (star) ──► center screw ──► C3410 GND pad
M2 slip-ring GND ──► end screw 5 ┘
```

- A **5-position brass grounding busbar** (all 5 screws = one common strip = one node) as the star hub. Cleaner than soldering two thin wires to the tiny `GND` pad; screw-terminal, swappable, single wire to the pad.
- **Still ONE shared net** — the busbar does not separate the mice (inherent to one headstage; won't cure cross-talk).
- **Build:** desolder greens from pad 5 (M1) + pad 13 (M2) → busbar; tin/fold wire tips so screws bite; center screw → C3410 `GND`; **DMM continuity screw1 ↔ center ↔ screw5 ↔ GND pad = ~0 Ω**; mount close to the headstage (short leads), keep the bar isolated.

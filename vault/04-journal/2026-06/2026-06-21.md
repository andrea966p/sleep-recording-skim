# Thread 1 — Grounding evidence pack 🔴

> [!abstract] Purpose
> Evidence for the professor: **the current wiring is grounded wrong**, and that — not the two-mice idea — is why the signal looks bad. Bring this before mouse grounding tomorrow. Full guide: [[signal-quality-and-grounding]]; bring-up: [[first-headstage-connection]].

**The claim, in one line:** the green **animal-ground wire is soldered to signal pad 5** on the C3410 (an amplifier **input**, not ground) → the animal is **floating**. That's the defect.

### Proof 1 — wrong pad, physically
- C3410 has **dedicated `REF` and `GND` pads**, separate from signal pads 0–31.
- Current build: green animal-ground → **signal pad 5** (native `pri_5`, an amplifier input) → measures voltage, doesn't tie the animal to chip ground → **no ground reference.**
- **Correct:** green on the **`GND` pad**, one animal-ground per setup.

### Proof 2 — live floating-animal signature
Signal Metrics, 2026-06-19 (Mouse 1, streaming):

| Channel | Reading | Healthy | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| EEG+ | ~60–100 µV | 20–300 µV | OK |
| EMG+ | ~60–100 µV | ~10–50 µV rest | OK-ish |
| **EMG−** | **~11.6 mV pk-pk, pegged at rail** | < ~1–2 mV | 🔴 saturated |
| green "ground" channel | **railing** | quiet | 🔴 floating |

Amplifier window = **±5 mV (~10 mV pk-pk max)**; ~11.6 mV pk-pk = slammed against the rail = unusable. The wire we *call* ground is itself railing = the textbook floating-animal fingerprint.

### Proof 3 — physics predicts it
RHD only sees **±5 mV**. No ground → the whole animal drifts out of that window → traces saturate + pick up 50 Hz hum. The bad signal is **predicted by the missing-ground wiring**, not by two-mice sharing.

### Proof 4 — it's a wire move, not a redesign (RHD2216)
Our chip = **RHD2216**, **bipolar**: each channel measures its own `+ − −` pair → it's its own reference → **no reference-electrode input** (the `REF` pad is for unipolar RHD2132 → **leave `REF` empty**). It **does** need **GND** → fix = **move green from pad 5 → `GND`**. Ref: [[intan-rhd2216-reference]].

> [!important] Can't judge the two-mice setup yet
> While channels saturate from a missing ground, **saturation hides everything** — cross-talk between mice can't be fairly evaluated. **Ground first, then look.**

### Tomorrow's order (after professor sign-off)
1. **Move green → `GND` pad** on C3410 + reflow messy joints ([[first-headstage-connection]] §7).
2. Select **sites 1–8** (`2mice32` map; Mouse 2 = sites **5–8**, not 9–12 — selection trap, [[signal-quality-and-grounding]] §1).
3. Re-look right: **Paired Reference** (EEG+ − EEG−, EMG+ − EMG−), 50 Hz notch, no saturation.
4. **Cross-talk nudge test:** move Mouse 1 only → Mouse 2 should not move → answers the two-mice question.

# 2026-10-20: Pinnacle acquisition PC, custom build decided

Context: [[2026-10-16-pinnacle-wireless-16-mice-evaluation]] · [[2026-10-19]]

## Decision (Andrea)
- **16 head units in total: 12 before the end of 2026, 4 more in January 2027.**
- **Custom build, not a Lenovo/HP/Dell business tower** (those cost roughly ₩0.5-1 M more for the same parts; the gain is
  warranty and procurement, not performance). Pinnacle's 9001-K1-AW workstation rejected (unnecessary).
- **One PC with 4 PCIe USB cards, 4 dongles per card = 16.** If it proves unstable, build a second identical PC and move
  2 cards to it (8 + 8). Sirenia Acquisition is free on any number of PCs (catalog), so the split costs only hardware.
- **DDR4 is fine.**

## Board (verified)
**ASUS PRIME B550-PLUS** ([ASUS tech spec](https://www.asus.com/motherboards-components/motherboards/prime/prime-b550-plus/techspec/)):
ATX, AM4, DDR4 4 x DIMM max 128 GB, no WiFi/Bluetooth (wired Realtek RTL8111H only), 8 rear USB.
Slots: 1 x PCIe 4.0 x16 (CPU) + 1 x PCIe 3.0 x16 (x4 mode, chipset) + **3 x PCIe 3.0 x1** = 5 slots
= 1 GPU + 4 USB cards. Most DDR4 ATX boards have only 4 slots (x16, x16@x4, 2 x x1), checked 10-20.
Alternative with 6 slots: MSI B550 GAMING GEN3 (2 x PCIe 3.0 x16 + 4 x PCIe 2.0 x1, per B&H; manufacturer page not read).

Rules that follow from 5 slots:
- **Single-slot graphics card** (e.g. GT 1030 2 GB), otherwise a dual-slot cooler covers an x1 slot.
- **USB cards must be PCIe x1** (one card fits in the x16@x4 slot). 4 ports, ONE controller per card, SATA/Molex aux power.
  The 4-controller StarTech PEXUSB3S44V is PCIe x4: it does not fit the x1 slots; not used in this plan.
- Physical slot order (which x1 sits under the GPU) NOT checked: read the manual before assembly.

## Update (later 10-20, Andrea): 3 cards + rear ports
4 dongles on the board's rear USB (through the 9052 extensions, away from the USB 3 noise) + **3 x PCIe x1 cards** x 4 = 16.
Keyboard/mouse on a small hub in a front port. Dongle data is tiny, so sharing the onboard controllers is fine; the spare
5th slot takes a 4th card if the rear-port mice drop out more in the December 12-mouse test. Any DDR4 ATX board with
4 slots (x16 + x16@x4 + 2 x x1) now qualifies; still a single-slot GPU.

## Hubs instead of cards? (discussed)
1 hub x 16 rejected: single point of failure (one hub chip or power brick ends all 16 runs). **2 powered hubs x 8**
acceptable: each on a different controller (rear port + 1 PCIe card), power brick on the UPS, **multi-TT**, metal/industrial,
plugged straight into the PC (no chaining). Dongles on short extensions so 16 radios are not side by side.
Ask Pinnacle: hubs supported? USB speed of the 8274-D (full-speed through a single-TT hub = shared 12 Mbit/s)?

## Pinnacle ships its own hub (checked)
**9005 Powered USB hub** is listed in Pinnacle accessory kits (catalog 2026 p.6 wireless biosensor, p.7-9 biosensor,
p.14 tethered EEG) and, per the store check of 10-16, in the wireless mouse accessory kit **8200-K10-SL/SE**. So hubs are
an expected setup. 9005 specs (ports, USB 2/3, multi-TT) are NOT published. Wait for the quote: if 9005s come with the
kits, use them, 4 dongles per hub, each hub on its own controller, power bricks on the UPS. Ask Pinnacle: ports per 9005,
dongles per hub, multi-TT? How many accessory kits come with the 8474-HR-SE4-C quote?

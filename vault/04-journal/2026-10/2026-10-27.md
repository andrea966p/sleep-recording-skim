# 2026-10-27: Pinnacle reply, Morelia and firmware read

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> - Pinnacle (Sam Kaplan) answered the 2026-10-26 email: TTL Plus alignment to wireless EEG no better than about ±78 ms,

## Rig expansion: Pinnacle
- Pinnacle's written reply filed, with each answer checked against the installer read: [[2026-10-27-pinnacle-reply-software-ttl-headunit-pc]]
- Morelia: same ±465.4 µV scaling and 244-byte packet as Sirenia; Linux through pyserial; PC-clock timestamps; no
  wireless TTL. 4-channel 8474HR support only on the unreleased `develop` branch, with a samples-per-packet bug.
- Firmware 0.4.1 is the legacy 3-channel 8274, not the 8474. The dongle has 4 connection slots (Sirenia uses one per
  dongle). Every packet carries a 16-bit head-unit clock tick that neither program reads: a possible drift-correction
  route for our own decoder. No buffering on dropout, no TTL, no battery reporting.
- Pinnacle's `pypvfs` package documents reading `.pvfs` on Linux; untested.
- Four new questions for Pinnacle added. [[2026-10-26-sirenia-3-3-5-installer-static-analysis]]

## Recording rig
- **Ground gate:** the 800 µV gate is to be replaced by measuring the current (post-fix) grounds;
  the −1.90 floor-slope cut gets redesigned. [[_eeg-analysis-hub]]
- **Allego:** stays without `--disable-gpu`; no Chan-height test. `rig-check.sh` now warns if the
  flag is present, and the procedures say so. Later the lab plans to move from Allego to the
  Open Ephys GUI with the XDAQ, a big switch for the recorder code and the daemon. [[_allego-hub]]
- **Grounding:** the Gen-1 has no separate Sys GND, only the chassis GND. The foil busbar is still
  not fitted. Planned instead: a GND bus, with the headstages (in their 3D-printed plastic
  enclosures) inside a closed aluminium box and a wire from the box to the bus; where the bus
  connects (perhaps the metal rack) is open. [[_eeg-rig-hub]]
- **Noise:** no more long tests chasing a ghost; the noise is considered fixed. [[_noise-hunt-hub]]
- **Pad 7 test:** closed without being run. [[experiment-pad7-floating-input-B1]]
- **Schedules:** recording stays global; several people must be able to queue runs for following
  dates. The console already accepts that; refusing overlapping bookings is the open item.
  [[_recording-console-hub]]

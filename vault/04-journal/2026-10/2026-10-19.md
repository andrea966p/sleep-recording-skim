## Verified today on Pinnacle's catalog 2026
- p.10 wiring diagram: "2 EEG + 2 independent" is Pinnacle's **4-channel standard configuration**:
  CH1 = EEG1 − COMMON, CH2 = EEG2 − COMMON, CH3 and CH4 differential, plus 1 COMMON and 1 GROUND = **8 pins, no spare**.
  Our use: CH3 = pons LFP target − LFP reference, CH4 = EMG A − EMG B. No custom pin order needed.
- I first proposed a "spare = 2nd GND" pin. **Wrong for this unit**: two ground pins exist only in the 3-channel fully
  independent layout. Retracted.
- p.13 photos: the headmount connector is a **female socket, openings up**; its legs stick out sideways (8415, "straight")
  or are bent under (-SM). The head unit plugs on from above.
- Headmounts page: 8415 = 8 pins, 5 x 3 mm; 8431-SM = 8 pins, 5 x 5 mm, "2 EEG/1 EMG (fully independent)".
  No pitch is published anywhere. 1.27 mm is inferred (8 pins only fit on 5 x 3 mm at that pitch).

## Headmount concept drawing
`06-references/attachments/2026-10-19-custom-headmount-concept.png` / `.svg`, made by
`06-references/attachments/headmount_drawing.py` (to scale, mm; edit the numbers at the top and re-run).
Board 7.0 mm ML x 5.6 mm AP, 2 x 4 socket with its long axis ML ("horizontal", per Andrea), one pair per column:
front row COMMON, EEG 1, LFP +, EMG A; back row GND, EEG 2, LFP −, EMG B; each pin to a plated via at the edge,
LFP wires through their vias and down to the pons. First version (hand SVG) had overlapping text; replaced.

**Redesign (later 10-19), in skull coordinates.** Electrode pins now protrude from the PCB at stereotaxic sites, following
our professor's protocol (Iwasaki 2021, [[tsukuba-sleep-recording-method]]: pins ML ±1.27, pairs 5.03 mm apart, posterior
pair just anterior to lambda, **no cerebellar screw**): EEG 1 = frontal L, COMMON = parietal L (so CH1 = the Tsukuba
bipolar EEG), EEG 2 = frontal R (optional), GND = parietal R; pons pair through one hole at AP −5.4, ML 0.9 (Schott 2023),
tips 1 mm apart; EMG pads at the back edge. Bregma–lambda taken as 4.2 mm (reference value; scale AP to the measured
distance). Board grows to 6.4 ML x 7.8 AP mm, over the 6 mm first given (Andrea: that number may be wrong).

**Redrawn properly (end of day).** The AI schematic skill was rejected (AI images, not to scale, invented labels).
The script now draws a hand-routed **2-layer board** with an automatic clearance check (trace 0.15 mm, clearance
≥ 0.15 mm: PASS), opaque board, Okabe-Ito colours per net, labels outside with non-crossing leaders.
**LFP target and LFP reference are now separate pins with their own pads** (target AP −5.4 ML 0.8, reference 1 mm
lateral, both at DV ≈ 4.5; reference placement to confirm with ahyeon). EMG pads moved behind the socket footprint.

## 16 mice on one PC (discussion)
Data rate is trivial (~66 kB/s for 16). Limits: dongles spread over 2 USB controllers on powered hubs, USB 2.0 ports,
UPS, no Windows auto-restart, USB selective suspend off. Interference = 2.4 GHz: the 16 links themselves, WiFi
(router to 5 GHz), other Bluetooth, USB 3.0 noise, metal/foil, water and bodies, distance.

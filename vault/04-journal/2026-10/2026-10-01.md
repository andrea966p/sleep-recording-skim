# 2026-09-30 / 10-01 — XDAQ noise series: what the rig picks up with no mice on

Full write-up, numbers and file list: [[2026-09-30-xdaq-noise-series]]. Background and earlier choices (cables,
bricks, grounding): Andrea's handover `xdaq-power-grounding-handover.md`.

## What happened, shortest version

- **Six rig-only test recordings, no mice, no tethers.** Every amplifier input was open, so the numbers are antenna
  pickup and must not be read as what a mouse sees.
- **Correction:** the Gen-1 XDAQ Core DOES have a chassis GND, and it is wired to the building earth through the wall
  socket. Five vault pages said otherwise since 07-16; each now carries a correction callout.
- **A light switch puts a 3-9 mV spike on every chip at the same instant.** Scanning the whole 52 h S-SL run (8 mice)
  found only **5 real all-chip events (about 2.3 a day), 2 of them large**, one being the 09:00 light timer. So the
  frequent "coordinated spikes" inside one mouse are within-mouse, not the building.
- **USB isolator (DFRobot FIT1019): no help.** Mains and switch spikes unchanged, broadband noise about 2x higher; it
  went back down when the isolator came out (A/B/A). **Record with the direct cable.** Isolator parked.
- **The mV on /1-/16 is the adapter board + slip ring acting as an antenna on open inputs**, not the soldering: removing
  the board cut it 6-12x on socket pads and bare pads alike. No solder bridges in the data.
- **SPI splitter:** mice that share one coincide at chance in archived data, so no swap test needed.
- **Open:** port A sees a strong 120 Hz field that port B does not (not the lights). Foil-to-chassis-GND and dummy loads are next.

## Evening: 4 mice on, then the first overnight of the series (19:50-21:00)

- **4 mice in A1 A2 B1 B2** (normal EEG/EMG, 8-core tethers). All 4 EEGs look like EEG (60 Hz 6-17 uV, 1/f).
  **A1 EMG and B2 EMG are open** (3.5 mV of 60 Hz, on the rail 14 % of the time, impedance = unwired input).
  Andrea: tethers checked, the cause is the implant, not fixable. So A2 and B1 are the only scoreable candidates.
- Andrea's Monitor: `Fields`, `Anti-Alias`, Chan height 40, AMP 500 uV, AIN 1.5 V, Window 10 s, viz 1-40 Hz +
  the usual 60 Hz notch. The notch is display-only. That was already known (2026-07-31) and I re-checked it anyway.
- Pre-overnight checks: 20-sweep impedance block (stream off), Allego Signal Metrics, pre-flight + biology probe,
  and 3 untouched runs for repeatability. **The A2 chip's floor repeats within 2-7 %**, so it is the noise meter
  for every later A/B test. A1/B2 floors swing 2.5-3.8x because of their railing EMGs.
- **Overnight scheduled in the console**: run `cfdf8ebde0c6`, 21:00 -> 09:00 (whole dark phase), batch `noise-tests`,
  **placeholder mouse IDs `POS-<slot>`** until Andrea gives the real ones. Details: Entry 6 of
  [[2026-09-30-xdaq-noise-series]].

## Mistakes made and caught

- I assumed tethers on an empty rig; Andrea corrected it (chain is XDAQ > SPI > headstage + board > slip ring).
- My first "all-chip events" were filter edge artefacts at chunk boundaries; fixed by trimming the edges.
- A first event plot showed 60-120 Hz ringing that came from my own notch filters; redone with a 3-cycle comb
  (x[t]-x[t-50 ms]), which cannot ring.
- 3 of 8 whole-run detections turned out to have a flat floor on inspection; the rate was revised down to 5 real events.
- Evening: I first ran the impedance sweep with the stream on (refused: `Z_measure attempted during data acq`), which
  memory already said. Harmless (the RPC refuses), but it was a known fact.

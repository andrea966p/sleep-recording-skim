# 2026-10-29: QC and strip may share the disk with another port

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> - Andrea: the console's QC bundle and strip of a per-port run may run while another port is recording, because per-port files are about 7.5 times smaller than the old combined file.
> - They still wait until the run's own files have stopped growing. The per-mouse export, manual strips, old combined-file runs and the archive jobs keep the old rule.
> - Code changed and tested. Andrea restarted `eeg-recorder` and `neurosurveillance-session` at 09:15; the console is healthy on the new code, the session daemon connected, and the old-client token errors stopped. [[2026-10-28-per-port-console-port]]

## Decision and change

- Decision recorded in [[2026-10-28-per-port-console-port]] (post-processing section) and as an exception to the rule in [[xdat-channel-stripping]] section 5b and [[_recording-console-hub]].
- Code: `postprocess.may_share_disk(run)` is true for per-port runs. QC calls `rig_busy(..., own_only=True)` and the strip gets `--ignore-other-recordings`, which skips other runs' files and the mid-strip re-check but still judges the run's own files by growth. Two new tests (`t_per_port_runs_may_share_the_disk_old_runs_may_not`, `t_own_only_ignores_other_runs_but_not_its_own_growth`); all suites pass.

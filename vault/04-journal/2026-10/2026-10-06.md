## SOD1 × deprivation quick compute
- **Design check first.** SOD1 or water starts at tethering (intake grid, Day 1), so every EEG baseline is about a week ON
  treatment and before CSD. Baseline therefore tests SOD1 without deprivation. Recovery − baseline is CSD (SD batches) or
  time (SL batches), on matched hours 13:00 → 09:00, because the SD recoveries only exist then.
- **Result:** [[2026-10-06-SOD1-sleep-EEG-2x2-quick-compute]].
  - No detectable SOD1 effect without deprivation: 61.1 vs 60.6 % of 24 h, n = 3 vs 6, p = 0.96; notched 56.2 vs 53.7 %, p = 0.24.
  - The SD question is not answerable from the EEG: S-SD has no scoreable recovery under either staging.
  - HLBWSD08 shows the CSD rebound, +12 to +16 points (n = 1).
- **EMG notch sensitivity run on e10** (rig idle, nothing scheduled): `qc/emg_mains_staging_test.py` on the eight window
  folders, `orig` reproduces 42/42 official verdicts. HLBSSD18 becomes scoreable at the S-SD baseline but not at recovery.
  The official W-SL baseline's 44–64 % dark-phase sleep is mains (notched 26–41 %).
- Code: `eeg-emg-analysis/qc/sod_sleep_2x2_2026_10_06.py`. Hub updated: [[_HLB-SOD1-project-hub]].

## S-SD EMG rescue (afternoon)
Andrea: "pick at least a few mice from the sleep deprivation SOD group and try to still score them … at least 1", then "I don't want EEG only, there should be EMG … further processing the EMG of those mice which look borderline".
- An EEG-only route was started and dropped on that instruction; its script was deleted.
- **Method:** EMG power between the 60 Hz harmonics (67–470 Hz) from the RAW 1000 Hz file, with stage 3 otherwise unchanged. The gate is stage 1's gate minus its EMG-mains clause. Applied to every mouse in all four batches, after a stage 2 `--all-verdicts` re-split on e10 (rig idle).
- **Validated:** κ 0.95 median (min 0.89, 33 windows) against the notched staging. The shuffle control rejects 57/57.
- **Result:** [[2026-10-06-S-SD-EMG-rescue-between-harmonics]]. **HLBSSD15 is scoreable at both timepoints**, so S-SD paired n = 1. Rebound +6.6 against water HLBWSD08's +16.1, interaction −13.8 points. It is a case comparison: HLBSSD15 had the highest baseline of all. No SOD1 effect without deprivation, now n = 8 vs 10 (p = 0.34).
- The first rescue run's gate (rung 2's EEG mask) fragmented windows and lost two good ones. It was replaced, and this is disclosed on the page.
- e10: outputs in `~/eeg-ssd-allverdicts-2026-10-06/` (9.7 GB). The temporary script copies were removed from e10's checkout, which is clean.

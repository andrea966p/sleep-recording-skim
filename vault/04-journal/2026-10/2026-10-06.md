# 2026-10-06: the building electrician inspects the recording room

The building electrician visited the recording room, with the 4 noise-series mice on the rig. It was an inspection only, with no recording.
The XDAQ was unplugged for his checks and plugged back in. Nothing changed with the mice (Andrea); e10 reads `XDAQ_ONE_REC`, 1000 Hz, USB 5000M.

**What will be done**
- **The rig socket will be replaced with an isolated-earth socket**, by the electricians. They switch the breaker off themselves, which
  takes down e10 and the XDAQ. Cost: the socket only. The quote and date are arranged by our lab.
- **The headstage foils will be joined to one earth busbar.** Each foil can be added or removed by us, for A/B/A tests.

**His reading**
- Every earth in the room is shared (the panels and all sockets), and motor loads (2nd-floor HVAC, the AHU room, 3rd floor) can push noise through it.
- The July ring terminal on the XDAQ earth wire is a generic part.
- He would rather not spend much, because the lab is moving to E7.

## Verdict on the noise hunt: [[2026-10-06-noise-hunt-verdict]]
Andrea's question: once the per-mouse faults are taken away, is the noise hunt chasing a ghost? Can post-processing remove what is left?
- **EEG:** the mains hum (2–9 µV) is outside the 0.5–30 Hz band that staging reads, so it does not matter.
- **EMG:** the 60 Hz mains is the only noise that hurts (A2 13–33 µV, the size of its quiet tone). A 60/120 Hz notch removes it, and
  that is validated on 100 recordings: scoreable 44 → 69, shuffle 0/100, dark > light wake 47/49.
  **Not adopted yet: it re-scores published numbers, Andrea's decision.**
- **Building spikes:** a few a day, handled by epoch rejection.
- **Recommended:**
  - do the cheap electrician work (socket, foil busbar), then stop the hunt;
  - skip the expensive earthing, the boxes and the Faraday cage;
  - put the effort into per-mouse reliability.
- **Not every EEG recording is clean:** before the 08-08 ground re-pin the hum was in mV. Every recording is still judged by its QC verdict.

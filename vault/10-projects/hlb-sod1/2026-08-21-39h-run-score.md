## 🔴 The floor bug, which changes every number on this page

`core/criteria.py` computes the wire-free floor by POOLING all 112 unwired inputs across all eight
chips. The project's own definition, in the same file, says the comparison is against

> "amplifier inputs with no wire attached, recorded simultaneously **through the same chip**, cable
> and room in the same file"

The chips are not interchangeable in this recording:

| chip | its own slope p1 | its floor median RMS | its floor median 60 Hz |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | **-2.079** | **1237 µV** | **1275 µV** |
| the other seven | -0.68 to -1.35 | 3451 to 4063 µV | 4336 to 5333 µV |

Pooling gives -1.633, which is **harsher than seven chips' own floors and more lenient than the
eighth**. The effect is not marginal:

| position | usable EEG, per-chip floor | usable EEG, pooled floor | longest block per-chip | pooled |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 99.9% | 41.5% | 457.5 min | 9.3 min |
| A2 | 99.1% | 31.7% | 252.0 min | 7.5 min |
| B2 | 92.4% | 23.7% | 261.5 min | 7.7 min |
| C1 | 97.7% | 21.6% | 211.2 min | 5.5 min |
| D2 | 98.5% | 29.4% | 86.2 min | 7.5 min |

**Under the pooled floor every single position fails the 15 minute block gate.** Under the per-chip
floor, five pass it. The floor is not a detail; on this recording it is the entire answer.

> [!warning] This is a bug in the shared code, not a setting
> `criteria.floor_stats()` still pools. Everything published from it on a recording where the chips
> differ is affected. The scoring on this page uses a per-chip floor computed alongside it.
> **Fixing `floor_stats()` properly, and re-checking earlier conclusions that used it, is owed.**

### Why C2 and D1 must not be read as sleep architecture

Their numbers look like architecture and are not. **`r@1min` of 0.11 and 0.001 is barely
distinguishable from randomly relabelled data**; the bar is 0.30. The architecture gives the same

epoch EXCLUDED.
✅ **B2's EEG columns remain valid for all 35,706 epochs** (`eeg_rms_uv`, `delta`, `theta`,
`theta_delta`) and are fine for spectral work.

## 🔴 FALSIFICATION ROUND — and it cost two animals

The findings above were put to an adversarial review, which proposed three tests. All three were
run. **Two animals did not survive, including the one with the strongest number on the rig.**

### Test 1 — does the criterion pass its own floor?

Apply the per-chip criterion to the 112 **wire-free** inputs themselves. If it were measuring chip
self-similarity rather than physiology, unwired channels would "pass".

**0 of 112 pass the 15-minute gate; median 0.00% usable.** The criterion survives. This also refutes
the specific charge that a per-chip floor "grades sick chips on a curve".

### Test 2 — can pure interference clear the bar?

Run the identical EMG gate but substitute a **wire-free channel's amplitude** for the EMG.

**16 of 112 wire-free channels reach r <= -0.30.** 🔴 **The fixed -0.30 bar is crossable by
interference alone and should not be used as an absolute threshold.** Replacing it with each chip's
own 14-channel null distribution separates the cohort cleanly:

| pos | r | z vs own chip's null | |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | -0.733 | -3.14 | below its own null |
| A1 | -0.580 | -2.96 | below its own null |
| C2 | -0.689 | -2.35 | below its own null |
| A2 | -0.525 | -2.12 | below its own null |
| D2 | -0.485 | -1.68 | below its own null (weakest survivor) |
| B2 | -0.203 | -1.37 | INSIDE its null |
| D1 | -0.075 | -0.53 | INSIDE its null |
| B1 | -0.023 | +0.29 | INSIDE its null |

B1, B2 and D1 falling inside their own nulls is a genuine negative control, and it agrees with the
hardware verdicts reached independently.

### Test 3 — is it arithmetic rather than physiology? THE DECISIVE ONE

**The delta RATIO carries EMG-band power in its denominator.** Broadband muscle or movement leaking
into the EEG channel inflates that denominator, so the ratio falls mechanically whenever EMG rises —
which would produce a strong negative r **in a dead animal**. Absolute delta power (µV²) has no
denominator and cannot do this.

| pos | r (delta ratio) | r (ABSOLUTE delta µV²) | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** | -0.580 | **-0.705** | **SURVIVES — strengthens** |
| **A2** | -0.525 | **-0.686** | **SURVIVES — strengthens** |
| **D2** | -0.485 | **-0.661** | **SURVIVES — strengthens** |
| B2 | -0.203 | -0.269 | weakened, still fails |
| **C1** | **-0.733** | **-0.009** | 🔴 **COLLAPSES** |
| **C2** | -0.689 | **+0.595** | 🔴 **COLLAPSES AND REVERSES SIGN** |
| B1 | -0.023 | +0.061 | collapses |
| D1 | -0.075 | +0.059 | collapses |

**The three survivors get STRONGER without the denominator, which is the opposite of what the
artefact predicts.** That is the strongest evidence in this whole report.

### ⛔ C1 AND C2 ARE WITHDRAWN

**C1 had the highest r on the rig (-0.733) and it was the ratio artefact.** Its inverted circadian
EMG was the warning sign, and it was correct. **C2** collapses and reverses, consistent with the
+0.812 correlation between its own two channels.

🔑 **Three independent lines — circadian direction, inter-channel correlation, and absolute delta
power — converge on the same three animals and kill the same two.**

> [!warning] The lesson, recorded because it will recur
> The animal with the best number was the one that was wrong. Both withdrawn animals had ALREADY
> been contradicted by an independent check before the decisive test was run, and the temptation was
> to treat those contradictions as puzzles to be explained rather than as verdicts. **A strong
> number that disagrees with a simple physiological check is not a mystery; it is a warning.**

Measured, not assumed. The bug only bites when a recording's chips actually differ, so all four

| 2026-08-08 ground-fix, 16.39 h | -1.328 | -1.540 .. -0.800 | 0.740 | yes |

**All four diverge, and in all four the pooled value is too harsh for exactly 7 of 8 chips and too
lenient for exactly 1.** So the error has a consistent direction:

> 🔑 Where the pooled floor was **harsher** than a chip's own: a **FAIL is suspect**, a **PASS is
> conservative and survives**. Only the single too-lenient chip per run has suspect passes
> (D1 on the two August runs, B1 on the two W-SD runs).

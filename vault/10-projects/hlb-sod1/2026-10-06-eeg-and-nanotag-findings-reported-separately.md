# SOD1 and sleep: EEG/EMG and nanotag findings are reported separately

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> 🛑 When summarising SOD1 effects on sleep, the **EEG/EMG batches** (W-SD, S-SD, W-SL, S-SL) and the **nanotag
> batches** (HLBNSL, NS-SD / NW-SD) are separate findings. No "the nanotag agrees", no nanotag number used to downgrade
> or support an EEG hint, and no nanotag value as the plausibility reference for EEG staging.
> Andrea, 2026-10-06: "nanotag findings are separated from eeg findings for SOD effect on sleep".

## Why
They are different methods (EEG/EMG staging vs immobility on an implanted accelerometer), different mice and different
protocols. On 2026-10-06 the EEG pages had first said "the nanotag batch agrees" and used nanotag bout lengths to call
an EEG consolidation hint unconfirmed; Andrea asked for the two to be kept apart.

## How to apply
- One section per method, each with its own n and its own caveats.
- Plausibility checks for EEG come from EEG data or the literature, never from the nanotag.
- A project-level summary ([[_hlb-sod1-hub]]) may list both side by side, never pooled.

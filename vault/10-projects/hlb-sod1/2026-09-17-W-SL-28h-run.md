> **Question.** Does the W-SL endpoint record cleanly when the recorder console runs it unattended?

## Known before the start
- **A1 EEG dead, its EMG fine** (2026-09-16, two tests on 5 min of live stream): EEG rms 6.2-6.3 µV against
  `DEAD_CHANNEL_UV` 20, slope −0.02; EMG 294-312 µV with 60 Hz at 15.9-40.6 µV, inside `CONNECTED_HUM_BAND_UV`.
  `r(E,M)` 0.00-0.01 on all eight. Only the EEG conductor is open. Expect 7 animals with EEG.
- **Mesa GPU crash risk accepted deliberately:** hardware GL kept (no `--disable-gpu`), so a 28 h unattended run was
  exposed to the crash that killed a recording on 2026-08-24 ([[starting-the-rig-after-a-restart]]).

## Result
- **File:** stop 13:00:05, 28.00 h, 100,793,088 samples, remainder 0 (card rung 0: PASS).
- **Workflow:** strip 262 → 16 ch (93.18 % saving), `verify: full` compared every sample, `exact: True`. Upload of 5
  files verified by sha256 at both ends, ~105 MB/s, with the QC bundle so the wire-free floor survives as a cached
  feature set. Landed at `andrea/20260917_HLBW_SL_RSR/`. Stop → verified on the NAS in 13 minutes.
- **Sleep, full 28 h, cards of 2026-09-26:** B1 / HLBWSL23 rung 3 PASS, r@1min 0.671, wake 38.0 %; B2 / HLBWSL24
  PASS, r@1min 0.697, wake 41.7 %. C1 (chip floor 5 µV) and D2 (floor carrying signal) are UNJUDGEABLE at rung 2.
  Wake of 33-42 % over a full cycle is normal; the 22-23 % quoted from the light phase alone is not comparable.
- **Superseded figure:** on 2026-09-18 D2 was also counted scoreable (r@1min 0.503, wake 33.5 %); the 2026-09-26 floor
  validity fix turned its rung 2 into UNJUDGEABLE ([[2026-09-18-W-SL-light-phase-sleep]], re-card section).
- **Pairing:** only B2 / HLBWSL24 is judgeable and accepted on both 09-10 and 09-17 ⇒ n = 1
  ([[2026-09-18-W-SL-light-phase-sleep]]). On the 24 h windows: [[2026-09-30-scoreable-mice-on-the-24h-windows]].

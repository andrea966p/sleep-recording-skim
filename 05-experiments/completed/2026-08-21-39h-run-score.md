# Scoring the 39.7 hour run

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> `allego_1__uid0819-17-25-20`, 39.674 h, 8 rig positions, file INTACT with zero dropped samples.
> **Three positions are fully scoreable: A1, A2, D2.** One is EEG only (B2), two are dead (B1, D1),
> and two pass the muscle test but are contradicted by independent physiology (C1, C2).
>
> 🔴🔴 **THE BIG ONE: the EMG gate now PASSES.** Until today this project had demonstrated
> state-dependent muscle tone on exactly ONE channel ever, a control that was not in the publishable
> set. Here **four positions pass it**, three of them corroborated by two independent checks that use
> no threshold at all. **That is the thing that was blocking the blind-scoring kappa.**
>
> 🔴 **A bug in the scoring code was the reason it looked blocked before.** The floor was POOLED
> across all eight amplifier chips when this project's own written definition says it must come from
> **the same chip**. Fixing that moved A1 from 41.5% usable to 99.9%.

## 🔴 The floor bug, which changes every number on this page

`core/criteria.py` computes the wire-free floor by POOLING all 112 unwired inputs across all eight
chips. The project's own definition, in the same file, says the comparison is against

> "amplifier inputs with no wire attached, recorded simultaneously **through the same chip**, cable
> and room in the same file"

The chips are not interchangeable in this recording:

| chip | its own slope p1 | its floor median RMS | its floor median 60 Hz |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | **-2.079** | **1237 µV** | **1275 µV** |
| the other seven | -0.68 to -1.35 | 3451 to 4063 µV | 4336 to 5333 µV |

Pooling gives -1.633, which is **harsher than seven chips' own floors and more lenient than the
eighth**. The effect is not marginal:

| position | usable EEG, per-chip floor | usable EEG, pooled floor | longest block per-chip | pooled |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 99.9% | 41.5% | 457.5 min | 9.3 min |
| A2 | 99.1% | 31.7% | 252.0 min | 7.5 min |
| B2 | 92.4% | 23.7% | 261.5 min | 7.7 min |
| C1 | 97.7% | 21.6% | 211.2 min | 5.5 min |
| D2 | 98.5% | 29.4% | 86.2 min | 7.5 min |

**Under the pooled floor every single position fails the 15 minute block gate.** Under the per-chip
floor, five pass it. The floor is not a detail; on this recording it is the entire answer.

> [!warning] This is a bug in the shared code, not a setting
> `criteria.floor_stats()` still pools. Everything published from it on a recording where the chips
> differ is affected. The scoring on this page uses a per-chip floor computed alongside it.
> **Fixing `floor_stats()` properly, and re-checking earlier conclusions that used it, is owed.**

## Rung 2 -- CONNECTION

Gate is the **longest unbroken block of at least 15 minutes**, not the total fraction. STRICT means
EEG **and** EMG usable in the **same** epoch. EEG is judged by spectral slope, EMG by 60 Hz and
amplitude -- a slope test on EMG is a category error, muscle is broadband, and `criteria.usable()`
refuses it outright.

| pos | EEG % | longest EEG block | EMG % | both % | longest both block | STRICT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 99.9 | **457.5 min (7.6 h)** | 96.2 | 96.1 | 96.7 min | **PASS** |
| A2 | 99.1 | 252.0 min | 82.8 | 82.1 | 44.5 min | **PASS** |
| C1 | 97.7 | 211.2 min | 67.3 | 66.1 | 47.0 min | **PASS** |
| D2 | 98.5 | 86.2 min | 94.1 | 92.8 | 53.8 min | **PASS** |
| C2 | 31.1 | 20.7 min | 40.9 | 30.9 | 20.7 min | PASS (marginal) |
| B2 | 92.4 | 261.5 min | **0.0** | 0.0 | 0.0 | FAIL (EMG open at 427 µV) |
| D1 | 22.0 | 15.5 min | 6.5 | 3.1 | 2.5 min | FAIL |
| B1 | **0.0** | 0.0 | 0.9 | 0.0 | 0.0 | FAIL (railed 54% of epochs) |

**A1 held one unbroken usable block of 7.6 hours**, and was at 100% in every one of the 39 hourly
bins. That is the longest unbroken usable block this project has recorded.

⚠️ **D1 decayed during the run.** Hours 0-8 sit at 29-82% usable; from hour 9 onward it is mostly
0-28%. A channel that dies mid-recording, not one that was never connected.

## 🔴 Rung 3 -- THE EMG GATE, which has blocked this project for weeks

r = correlation over 1-minute bins between EMG amplitude and EEG delta ratio. Negative means the
muscle goes quiet when the brain goes delta-rich. Bar for PASS is r <= -0.30.

| pos | r all | r light | r dark | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | **-0.755** | -0.677 | **-0.815** | STATE-DEPENDENT |
| **A1** | **-0.580** | -0.448 | -0.565 | STATE-DEPENDENT |
| **A2** | **-0.524** | -0.375 | -0.486 | STATE-DEPENDENT |
| **D2** | **-0.488** | -0.448 | -0.355 | STATE-DEPENDENT |
| C2 | -0.389 | -0.270 | -0.411 | STATE-DEPENDENT |
| D1 | -0.128 | -0.112 | -0.157 | none |
| B2 | -0.066 | +0.065 | -0.061 | none |
| B1 | -- | -- | -- | INSUFFICIENT, no usable EEG |

For context, the previous best on an experimental animal was **-0.174**, and the only channel ever to
pass was a control at -0.734. **C1 at -0.755 beats that control, and it is not a control.**

### Two checks that could have killed this, and did not

**1. Does it depend on the floor fix?** No. Recomputed with **no EEG-usability restriction at all**,
so no floor and no threshold enter: A1 -0.580, A2 -0.525, C1 -0.733, D2 -0.485. Every conclusion is
unchanged. The gate result is not an artefact of the per-chip floor.

**2. Is the EMG just picking up the EEG?** Correlate the two channels' own amplitudes. A **positive**
correlation is the shared-artefact signature; a **negative** one is physiological, since NREM raises
EEG amplitude while muscle tone falls.

| pos | r(EEG rms, EMG rms) | reading |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | -0.673 | physiological |
| A2 | -0.525 | physiological |
| C1 | -0.388 | physiological |
| D2 | -0.323 | physiological |
| **C2** | **+0.812** | **SHARED PATH -- its gate result is not trustworthy** |

**3. Independent nocturnal check.** Mice are nocturnal, so raw EMG amplitude should be higher in the
dark. No threshold involved.

| pos | EMG light | EMG dark | dark/light | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A2 | 10.7 µV | 68.5 µV | **6.4x** | correct |
| A1 | 17.3 | 76.7 | **4.4x** | correct |
| D2 | 18.9 | 77.5 | **4.1x** | correct |
| C1 | 101.1 | 79.0 | 0.78 | **INVERTED** |
| C2 | 19.6 | 671.7 | 34x, and 672 µV is not muscle | implausible |

A1, A2 and D2 land at 10-19 µV in the light and 68-78 µV in the dark, which is the same range as the
old 48 h control (31 / 73 µV).

## What this unblocks, and what it does not

✅ **Three animals with simultaneous usable EEG and EMG, state-dependent muscle tone confirmed three
independent ways, over 39.7 h spanning two full dark phases.** The blind-scoring kappa now has
material. Rules are already pre-registered in `02-procedures/blind-scoring-and-kappa.md`.

🛑 **This is NOT a sleep result.** No hypnogram, no bout structure, no percentage asleep. Rung 4
stays PROVISIONAL until the kappa exists, and that rule does not bend because the data finally looks
good. 🛑 **Do not tune the staging threshold to maximise anything.**

## How far back does the floor bug reach

Measured, not assumed. The bug only bites when a recording's chips actually differ, so all four
substantive recordings were checked:

| recording | pooled p1 | per-chip range | spread | chips diverge? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-08 ground-fix, 16.39 h | -1.328 | -1.540 .. -0.800 | 0.740 | yes |
| 2026-08-09 A2 control, 48 h | -1.352 | -1.666 .. -0.840 | 0.826 | yes |
| 2026-08-18 W-SD screen, 1 h | -1.510 | -1.942 .. -0.407 | 1.535 | yes |
| 2026-08-19 this run, 39.7 h | -1.633 | -2.079 .. -0.676 | 1.403 | yes |

**All four diverge, and in all four the pooled value is too harsh for exactly 7 of 8 chips and too
lenient for exactly 1.** So the error has a consistent direction:

> 🔑 Where the pooled floor was **harsher** than a chip's own: a **FAIL is suspect**, a **PASS is
> conservative and survives**. Only the single too-lenient chip per run has suspect passes
> (D1 on the two August runs, B1 on the two W-SD runs).

### Re-running the 2026-08-08 EMG gate per-chip

That run is where "no experimental animal passes the EMG gate" came from, and it is why the kappa
was declared blocked. Recomputed on the same file with a per-chip floor:

| animal | r pooled | n min | r per-chip | n min | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B2** | -0.156 | 159 | **-0.366** | 203 | **crosses the bar → STATE-DEPENDENT** |
| A2 (control) | -0.734 | 597 | -0.713 | 983 | unchanged |
| C2 | -0.174 | 137 | -0.180 | 142 | unchanged |
| D1 | +0.023 | 428 | +0.028 | 326 | unchanged |
| D2 | +0.010 | 353 | -0.135 | 660 | improves, still fails |
| A1, B1, C1 | — | — | — | — | no usable EEG either way |

**So "no experimental animal passes" is false: B2 passes.** The broader claim survives — three of
the four evaluable experimental animals still fail.

⭐⭐ **This is the most important control on today's result.** The floor fix, applied to a recording
that genuinely lacked state-dependent EMG, moves exactly ONE animal. It does not manufacture passes.
**That is why the four passes on the 39.7 h run should be read as a real change in the data.**
